# Magic Eraser // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Magic Eraser, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on each flavor in turn: Classic Era, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N." This is Magic Eraser's first release on WoW Forever, so every step there is a first run.

Gather these first, so you are not caught short halfway through:

- **Characters.** On each flavor, one character at level 15 or higher who is **not** a Shaman. A second character on the same account for the Global list check in step 20, and a Shaman on the same account for step 18. For step 15 you want a character partway through one of the quest chains named there; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore for Alliance and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar for Horde both open before level 10. For step 16 you want a character who is locked out of a quest by race or class, and one who is not: any non-Human for *Goldshire Gift Voucher*, any non-Tauren for *Bloodhoof Village Gift Voucher*, any non-Rogue for *Elegant Letter*.
- **Lua errors on screen.** Type `/console scriptErrors 1` once on each client, or run BugSack, so no error passes silently.
- **A spare key.** One key you use for nothing else, to bind in step 9. Clear the binding when you finish, since a stray press erases.
- **Items and bags.** Two spare grays, each a single item rather than a stack. More gray trash with a sell price. A gray a vendor will not buy. A curated white, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*. An item your client draws in green. White trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*. *Refreshing Spring Water*, *Ice Cold Milk* and *Melon Juice*. A single stack of gray junk worth more than one gold. A stack of *Fish Oil*. A bound item that starts a quest and is already spent, meaning either the quest is done or your race or class can never take it. A quest item for a quest you have not finished. For step 4, a **Rare (blue) item** a vendor will buy, which that step only drops and cancels, and on WoW Forever a second Rare item you are willing to lose. For step 3, a bag sitting in one of your bank's bag slots. Keep a few free bag slots.
- **Guard your fixtures.** Every erase takes whatever the mini-map button names under **Lowest-Value Item**, which may be a fixture rather than a gray. Before any Left-Click or key press, check the name: if a later step still needs that item, Right-Click the button to ignore it, and Middle-Click to clear the Ignore List once the step is done. Only steps 21 and 22 sell, and they come after every step that needs a flagged fixture.
- **Location and state.** Out of combat except where a step says otherwise. A merchant and a bank you can reach, ideally close together, and something willing to fight you for step 10.
- **A non-English client**, for the optional last step only.

Every step below has a pass or fail you can see. To stay short enough to finish in one sitting, this plan deliberately skips the erase confirmation dialog beyond the one check in step 9, the welcome and mini-map toggles, profile switching, Restore Defaults on the Erase List, the Bag-Space Warnings countdown, Auto-Vend deferring a sale until combat ends, Validate Data on the other three data files, and the Event Log, Event Registration, Saved Variables and Library Versions reports.

## Verify this release's changes

Everything in this section is new since 2026.09.10.A, the last tagged release, so this is the part worth running carefully.

### One TOC per flavor

**1.** At the character select screen on each client, open **AddOns**. **Magic Eraser** must be listed once, enabled, and not marked out of date or incompatible, without ticking any option to load out-of-date add-ons. Log in, open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Diagnostic Tools**, turn on **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, and click **Show Eraser Context**. The report's first line must end `// Flavor Vanilla` on Classic Era, `// Flavor Forever` on WoW Forever, and `// Flavor TBC` on TBC Anniversary; on a Season of Discovery character, if you have one, `// Flavor Vanilla (Season of Discovery)`. Click **List Installed Add-ons** too: `MagicEraser` must be listed with its version and `[loadable]`. Failure is the add-on missing from any client's list, an out-of-date mark, a first line ending `Flavor ?` or naming the wrong flavor, a first line still reading `Project` followed by a number, or a Lua error from either button.

### Item reads rewired for WoW Forever

**2.** With a flagged gray in your bags, visit every place the add-on reads an item. Hover the mini-map button: **Lowest-Value Item** must show the item's icon, its link in its quality color, and its value. Hover the gray in your bags: its tooltip must end with a red `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.` line. Right-Click the button and hover it again: an **Ignore List** block must list that gray with its icon and its name drawn in gray. Middle-Click to clear it. Then open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Erase List**: every row must be an item link with its icon, and none may stay on `Loading ID:` for more than a moment. Failure is any Lua error, a missing icon, a name in the wrong color, or a row stuck on `Loading ID:`. **WoW Forever is the flavor that breaks here**: its engine has none of the older item functions, so any read this release missed errors there and nowhere else.

### Bank Retrieval and the bank layout readout

**3.** Put your two spare grays in your bank, one in its own slots and one in the bag sitting in a bank bag slot, and close it. Open it again: both must come back to your bags under one line, `Pulled 2 items (2 bag slots) out of your bank, worth X.` Then click **Show Eraser Context** in Diagnostic Tools. The report must carry a `Bank layout:` block, and at its end, under `Current erase candidate:`, one `tooltip =` line per line of that item's tooltip, the first naming the item. Failure is either gray left in the bank, no line in chat, a Lua error, a report missing either block, or a `tooltip = (tooltip read failed)` line. If a gray stays behind, paste the whole `Bank layout:` block into your report. **WoW Forever is the flavor to watch**: Bank Retrieval assumes the bank is laid out the way the other two clients lay it out, and the newer engine is where that is least certain.

### Manual Delete Assistance on WoW Forever

**4.** Open **Safety Features** and note the word the **Manual Delete Assistance** paragraph quotes. With that section on and **Applies To** on the shipped **No Sale Value**, drag your sellable Rare item out of your bags and drop it on the ground: the game's own prompt must appear untouched, asking you to type that same word, letter for letter and in the same capitals. Press **No**. Switch **Applies To** to **All Items** and drop the item again: this time the prompt must be a plain Yes or No, with no typing box, no line telling you to type anything, and a **Yes** button you can click. Press **No**, and the item must still be in your bags. On WoW Forever only, drop the Rare item you are willing to lose and press **Yes**: it must be gone. Set **Applies To** back to **No Sale Value**. Failure is a different word or different capitals than the game's prompt, the typing box surviving under All Items, a grayed-out Yes, a Lua error, an item gone after No, or a Yes that leaves the item behind. This has only ever been proven on Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, so **WoW Forever is the flavor where it may break.**

### Validate Data

**5.** In Diagnostic Tools, click **Validate Data.lua**. The box must count `Validated N / M` for a moment, then show a report whose second line reads `Data.lua // 2 item ids // 2 OK // 0 NOT ON CLIENT // 0 NOT LOADED`, followed by a header row and two rows, *Shiny Fish Scales* and *Fish Oil*, each reading `SHAMAN` under `DATA_CLASS`. Click **Validate Quest-Starting-Items.lua**: its second line must go on to count quest ids, ending `// N quest ids // N OK // N NO TITLE`, and the report must end with a second block, a header row starting `STATUS`, `SOURCE`, `QUEST_ID`, `TITLE`, then one row per quest. Paste that report into a spreadsheet: every row must line up under its header, and the item rows must end in `TOOLTIP_` columns holding each item's tooltip lines. Note any NOT ON CLIENT, NOT LOADED or NO TITLE count on each flavor for your report; a count above zero is data for the next release, not this step failing. Failure is a Lua error, a box stuck on its progress count, a missing quest block, or rows that fall out of line with the header.

### Mini-map position readout

**6.** Drag the mini-map button to a new spot around the mini-map's edge and let go. In Diagnostic Tools, click **Show Display Context**: `math.atan2 present` must read `true`, `Button db is ns.db.global.minimap` must read `true`, and `Button db.minimapPos` and `MagicEraserDB.global.minimap.minimapPos` must show the same number. `/reload`: the button must come back exactly where you left it, and once you turn **Enable Diagnostic Tools** back on, the report must show that same number. Log out, log back in, and check both once more. Failure is the button jumping back to an old spot, a Lua error while you drag, either line reading `false`, or the two numbers disagreeing. **WoW Forever, on the newer engine, is the flavor to watch.**

When steps 1 to 6 pass on every flavor, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**7.** Log in with the add-on freshly enabled. Exactly one branded line must print, naming a version and pointing at Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser. On an unpackaged development copy the version reads `Dev`, which is correct; a packaged release must read a real version number on every flavor. Then `/reload`. Failure is no message, a doubled message, a literal `%s` where the version belongs, or any Lua error on either load.

**8.** Reach the Options Interface all three ways. Type `/eraser`. Close it and hold Shift while Middle-Clicking the mini-map button. Close it again, open the Blizzard Options window yourself and pick **Magic Eraser** out of the category list. All three must land on the same docked page, and under the **/Commands** header `/eraser` must be listed in blue followed by **Opens the Options Interface for this add-on.** Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a run on Classic Era alone has not finished this step.

**9.** On the General page, under a **Key Bindings** header, **Erase Lowest-Value Item** must sit in blue, followed by a line saying it does exactly what left-clicking the mini-map button does, that you use it at your own risk, and where to set it. Follow those directions to the game's key binding list: it must hold a **Magic Eraser** section with exactly one entry, **Erase Lowest-Value Item**. Bind your spare key to it and `/reload`: it must still be bound. Hover the mini-map button, note the item under **Lowest-Value Item**, move the mouse away, and press the key. That item must go exactly as a Left-Click erases it: the erase sound, the same chat line a Left-Click prints with a working item link, and the button's icon moving on. Then open **Safety Features**, turn on **Mini-map Eraser Confirmation** with **For Gray Vendor-Trash Items** ticked, close the Options window, and press the key with a gray named: the same `Erase [item]?` dialog a Left-Click raises must appear. Click **No**, and the gray must still be in your bags. Turn the confirmation back off. Failure is no Magic Eraser section, a raw name such as `MAGICERASER_ERASE`, directions that do not lead you there, the binding gone after the reload, a different item going than the one named, or a key press erasing anything a Left-Click would have asked about first. **Follow the directions to the letter on WoW Forever**, the newest of the three clients, where they are most likely not to match the game's menus.

**10.** Get into combat and type `/eraser`. One line must read `Magic Eraser // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat.` and the panel must **not** open. Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button while still in combat: same line, same refusal. Ask three or four more times each way, and the line must print **every** time. Still in combat, with junk in your bags, Left-Click the button and then press your erase key: each must print `Magic Eraser // Cannot erase items while in combat.` and nothing may be erased. Then let combat end without touching anything: the panel must not open by itself and nothing may be erased late. Failure is the panel opening, an item vanishing, silence after the first refusal, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Magic Eraser.

**11.** Hover the mini-map button with junk in your bags. The tooltip must read, top to bottom: the add-on name with the version on the right; **Lowest-Value Item** with the item's icon, link and value; **Left-Click / Erase**; **Right-Click / Ignore**; **Auto-Vend** showing Enabled or Disabled, with its description and **Shift + Right-Click / Toggle**; **Clutter Report** with a bag-slot count, an item count and a total; the **Ignore List** with **Middle-Click / Clear Ignore List** below it, if you have anything ignored; and last, **Magic Eraser Options** with **Shift + Middle-Click**. The button's icon must match the item named under Lowest-Value Item. Failure is a missing block, a block out of that order, an empty line where a value belongs, or an icon that does not match.

**12.** Work through the clicks and confirm each does what the tooltip beside it says. Left-Click erases the named item, plays a sound, prints `Erased [item], worth [price].` with a working item link, and refreshes the tooltip to the next item **while you are still hovering**. Right-Click adds the named item to this character's Ignore List, and it appears in the tooltip's Ignore List block. Middle-Click empties that block. Shift + Right-Click flips the Auto-Vend line between Enabled and Disabled live, and **Enable Auto-Vend** on the General page must be flipped to match; leave it Enabled. Failure is any click doing something other than its label, the two Auto-Vend readings disagreeing, a broken link in the erase line, or a tooltip that catches up only after you move the mouse away and back.

**13.** With several flagged items of different values in your bags, confirm the candidate really is the cheapest, comparing whole stacks rather than single items: twenty grays at 1 copper is worth 20 copper and outranks a single 5 copper item. Then make two flagged items worth the same and check the tie-break, which runs Erase List items first, then completed quest items, then gray trash, then consumables and curated whites together. Failure is a more valuable stack being offered first, or a tie broken the other way.

**14.** Check the outgrown-consumable boundary, on one character you can level or on three at the right levels: *Refreshing Spring Water* is flagged from level 5, *Ice Cold Milk* from 15, *Melon Juice* from 25, and none of the three one level below that. Then carry a drink one level short of qualifying and **level up**: the candidate must update on the level-up itself, with you touching nothing. Failure is a boundary landing somewhere else, or having to move an item before the add-on notices.

**15.** Pick a quest chain that hands you an item a later quest still needs; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar are the quickest. Work it up to the quest **before** the last one that needs the item, turn that one in, and accept the next so the item is back in your bags, since several of these chains take the item at one turn-in and hand it straight back for the next. Stop there and wait a few seconds: chat must print **nothing** about the item, its tooltip must carry **no** Magic Eraser line, and it must never be named under **Lowest-Value Item** or wear its icon on the button. Type `/reload` and check all three again. Now turn in the last quest that needs it. If the item is still in your bags, within about a second chat must print exactly one line reading `[item] can now be safely erased!`, its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.`, and it must become the candidate; Left-Click must print `Erased [item], left over from a quest you have completed.` If the turn-in used the item up, silence is correct. Failure before that last turn-in is the dangerous one: it means the add-on is offering to erase a quest item the character is about to need.

**16.** Put a spent quest starter in your bank, `/reload`, and open the bank so Bank Retrieval hands it back; looting a fresh one works too. Within about a second of it landing in your bags, chat must print exactly one line: `[item] can now be safely erased!` when the quest is already complete, or `[item] can be safely erased. It starts a quest your character cannot take.` when your race or class is locked out. Move it between bag slots, then close and reopen the bank: it must **not** print again. Once it is the named candidate, Left-Click: chat must read `Erased [item], which starts a quest your character cannot take.` for a lock-out, and `Erased [item], left over from a quest you have completed.` for a quest you finished. Separately, on a character who **can** take that quest and has not done it, carry a copy of the same item: no alert, no tooltip line, never the candidate. Failure is silence, a repeat line on every bag change, the wrong sentence for the reason, or, worst of all, the eligible character being offered a quest starter it still needs.

**17.** Confirm what is flagged and what never is. Put a curated white in your bags, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*: its name must be white, and its tooltip must carry a red `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.` line. Then work through the never-touched set one item at a time: an item your client draws in **green**, white trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*, your equipped gear, a quest item for a quest you have not finished, and a gray a vendor will not buy. None may show a Magic Eraser line and none may ever be named under **Lowest-Value Item**; keep the bag items with you for step 21, where none may sell. Pick a trade good that is **not** on your Erase List, since the seeded fishing reagents are white trade goods the add-on is supposed to take. Run the green half on **every** flavor with the same item, because a handful of low-level crafted pieces are white on one client and green on another, and the client in front of you decides. Failure here is the most dangerous kind of bug this add-on can have.

**18.** Log in on a character that is **not** a Shaman and open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Erase List**. That character's own scope must already list **Shiny Fish Scales** and **Fish Oil**. Carry your *Fish Oil* stack: its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // Flagged by your Erase List.` in red. Click the **Global** button on Shiny Fish Scales: that row must leave this pane and turn up under **Global**. Remove it there with its remove icon: it must go with one click and no confirmation, and must not come back to either pane after a `/reload`. Log in on a Shaman and check the same pane: it must list **neither** reagent. Failure is an empty list on a fresh non-Shaman, a Fish Oil stack with no red line, a promoted row showing in both panes, a removed row coming back after a reload, or a Shaman starting with their own reagents listed.

**19.** Open **Safety Features** and turn on **Maximum Value to Erase**. Its dropdown must read **1 Gold, 2 Gold, 3 Gold, 5 Gold, 8 Gold, 13 Gold, 21 Gold** in that order; pick **1 Gold**, with your junk stack worth more than a gold in your bags. Without reloading, the stack's tooltip must lose its Magic Eraser line, the **Clutter Report** must drop by that stack, and the stack must never be named under **Lowest-Value Item** or wear its icon on the button. Now open the **Erase List**, click into **Add by Item ID**, Shift-click the stack in your bags, and press Enter: its row must appear, and the stack's tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // Flagged by your Erase List.` in red again, cap or no cap. Add it to the **Ignore List** the same way: the tooltip must flip to `Magic Eraser // Protected by your Ignore List.` in white. Remove it from both lists, and leave the cap on for step 21. Failure is "13 Gold" anywhere but between "8 Gold" and "21 Gold", the capped stack still offered or still counted, the cap hiding an item on your Erase List, or the Erase List winning over the Ignore List.

**20.** Right-Click the mini-map button to ignore the current candidate, then confirm it is skipped: its tooltip reads `Magic Eraser // Protected by your Ignore List.` in white, it never becomes the candidate, and put in your bank it stays there when you close and reopen the bank. Open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Ignore List**, find that row under this character and click its **Global** button: the row must leave this character's pane and appear under **Global**. Log in on a second character with its own Ignore List empty and carry the same item: it must still be protected. Keep it in your bags for step 21. Failure is a protected item being erased or retrieved on either character, or the promoted row showing up in both panes.

**21.** Take your bags to a merchant with **Enable Auto-Vend** and **Enable Auto-Vend Messages** on and the mode set to **Line Item**. Everything flagged that a vendor will buy must sell on opening, including the junk stack worth more than a gold with step 19's cap still on. Each sale prints one line reading `Sold [item], worth [price].` with a working, quality-colored item link you can click, and closing the merchant window prints one more line reading `Sold N items (M bag slots), worth X.` Left in your bags must be the item you ignored in step 20 and everything from step 17's never-touched set. Turn the cap back off. Failure is a broken or half-rendered link, a stray `%s`, a line that is not one complete sentence, the capped stack left unsold, or anything protected or never-touched sold.

**22.** Switch the mode to **Summary Only**, the shipped default. Buy one gray back from the merchant's **Buyback** tab, close the window (nothing prints, correctly, since nothing sold), and talk to the merchant again: on closing, only one line may print, `Sold 1 item (1 bag slot), worth X.` Do the same with your Fish Oil stack on its own: `Sold N items (1 bag slot), worth X.` Then check the bank's floor. On a fresh profile, with Bag-Space Warnings off, retrieval may use every free slot you have. Put more flagged items in your bank than you have free slots, turn **Enable Bag-Space Warnings** on with **Free-Slot Threshold** at 4, and open the bank: retrieval must stop with at least four slots still free. Failure is "1 items" or "1 bag slots", a per-item line in Summary Only mode, a sale with no closing line, or a bank that ignores the threshold once the warnings are on.

**23.** `/reload` and open **Diagnostic Tools**. **Enable Diagnostic Tools** must be **off**, with only the warning paragraph and that toggle visible. Turn it on and click **Test WoW API Endpoints**. Every line must read PASS, with three kinds of exception. Six pairs need only one half to pass, and a FAIL on the other half is the report working rather than a defect: `TooltipDataProcessor.AddTooltipPostCall` with `GameTooltip.SetBagItem (legacy)`, and `C_Item.GetItemInfo`, `C_Item.GetItemInfoInstant`, `C_Item.GetItemQualityColor`, `C_Item.GetItemClassInfo` and `C_Item.GetItemSubClassInfo`, each with the legacy row of the same name. On WoW Forever, the `C_Item` half of those five must be the half that passes. `C_QuestLog.GetTitleForQuestID` and `C_QuestLog.GetQuestInfo` need only one of the two. And `C_Item.GetItemSpell`, `C_Item.GetDetailedItemLevelInfo`, `C_Item.GetItemStats`, `C_TooltipInfo.GetItemByID` and `C_QuestLog.RequestLoadQuestByID` may FAIL, which only leaves some Validate Data columns blank. Note which rows failed, on each flavor. The four `StaticPopup1` rows must PASS, on WoW Forever above all, since step 4 depends on them, and no row may name `GetAddOnMetadata (legacy)` or `InterfaceOptionsFrame_OpenToCategory`. Then `/reload` and reopen the panel: the toggle must be back **off**. Failure is the toggle surviving a reload, both halves of a pair reading FAIL, both quest-title rows reading FAIL, or a FAIL on any row not named here.

**24.** Optional, on a non-English client: open the Options Interface, walk the General and Safety Features pages, find the **Magic Eraser** section in the game's key binding list, hover the mini-map button, and erase and sell one item. Every string must render as real words in that language, with no raw keys such as `TAB_SAFETY` showing through, and every number and item name must land inside its sentence rather than leaving a bare `%s` or `%d` behind. The General page's description is newly translated in every language and must now mention the mini-map button; on a Russian client the welcome line is new too and must print in full. The **Manual Delete Assistance** paragraph must quote the same word that client's own delete prompt asks for, and the closing line for selling one item must read naturally for a single item. Failure is a raw key, a stray placeholder, a sentence assembled in the wrong order, or a quoted word the client's own prompt does not use.

When every step passes on Classic Era, WoW Forever and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
