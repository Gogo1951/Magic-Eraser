# Magic Eraser // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Magic Eraser, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on each flavor in turn: Classic Era, WoW Forever, TBC Anniversary, and Season of Discovery on the Classic Era client.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these first, so you are not caught short halfway through:

- **The last release, for step 1.** On one flavor, keep **2026.10.02.A** installed until step 1 tells you to update.
- **Characters.** On each flavor, one character at level 15 or higher who is **not** a Shaman and has already played the last release; every step runs on this character unless it says otherwise. A free character slot on each flavor for step 5, and a Shaman on the same account for step 20. For step 17, a character partway through one of the quest chains named there; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore for Alliance and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar for Horde both open before level 10. For step 18, a character locked out of a quest by race or class, and one who is not: any non-Human for the Human starting area's Gift Voucher (*Northshire Gift Voucher* on Classic Era and WoW Forever, *Goldshire Gift Voucher* on TBC Anniversary and Season of Discovery), any non-Tauren for the Tauren one (*Camp Narache Gift Voucher* or *Bloodhoof Village Gift Voucher*), any non-Rogue for *Elegant Letter*.
- **Lua errors on screen.** Type `/console scriptErrors 1` once on each client, or run Bug Sack, so no error passes silently.
- **Three spare keys** for step 15, keys you use for nothing else. Clear the bindings when you finish, since a stray press of the erase key erases.
- **Items and bags.** Two spare grays, each a single item rather than a stack. More gray trash with a sell price, including a stack of three or more of one gray. A gray a vendor will not buy. A white weapon or armor piece a vendor will buy, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*. A white profession tool, *Mining Pick* or *Fishing Pole*, and a white shirt such as *Brown Linen Shirt*. An item your client draws in green. White trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*. *Refreshing Spring Water*, *Ice Cold Milk* and *Melon Juice*. *Rough Arrow*, *Sharp Arrow*, *Razor Arrow* and *Jagged Arrow*. A single stack of gray junk worth more than one gold. A stack of *Fish Oil*. A bound item that starts a quest and is already spent, meaning either the quest is done or your race or class can never take it. A quest item for a quest you have not finished. A **Rare (blue) item** a vendor will buy, which step 23 only drops and cancels. A bag sitting in your **first** bank bag slot. Keep a few free bag slots.
- **Guard your fixtures.** Every erase takes whatever the mini-map button names under **Lowest-Value Item**, which may be a fixture rather than a gray. Before any Left-Click or key press, check the name: if a later step still needs that item, Right-Click the button to protect it, and Middle-Click to clear the Protect List once the step is done. Only steps 21 and 22 sell or retrieve, and they come after every step that needs a flagged fixture.
- **Location and state.** Out of combat except where a step says otherwise. A merchant and a bank you can reach, ideally close together, and something willing to fight you for step 13.
- **A non-English client**, for the optional last step only.

Every step below has a pass or fail you can see. To stay short enough to finish in one sitting, this plan deliberately skips the welcome and mini-map toggles, profile switching beyond step 1, the All Characters lists, Your Current Bags beyond its Erase button, the example lines on Merchant & Bank and Alerts & Tooltips, Auto-Vend waiting out combat, the Arathi Basin and Warsong Gulch rations, the mini-map position readout, and the Event Log, Taint Log, Event Registration, Display Context, Saved Variables, Other Add-ons and Library Versions tools.

## Verify this release's changes

Everything in this section is new since 2026.10.02.A, the last tagged release, so this is the part worth running carefully.

### Settings carried over from the last release

**1.** Do this first, while **2026.10.02.A** is still installed. On **Erasing**, set **Gray Trash** to **Erase, Ask First** and **White Weapons & Armor** to **Keep**, leaving the other four on **Erase**. In **Profiles**, make a new profile named *Bank Alt*, set **Outgrown Arrows & Bullets** to **Keep** in it, and switch back to **Default**. Log out, update to this build, and log in. **Erasing** must show **White Weapons & Armor** unchecked, the other five kinds checked, and **Enable Erase Confirmation** on. Switch to *Bank Alt*: only **Outgrown Arrows & Bullets** may be unchecked, and Erase Confirmation must be off. Switch back to **Default**, `/reload`, and everything must read exactly as before the reload. Delete *Bank Alt*. Failure is a kind checked that you had set to Keep, a kind unchecked that you had left on Erase, a lost confirmation on Default, one appearing on *Bank Alt*, or anything changing after the reload.

### Junk kinds as checkboxes, and Erase Confirmation

**2.** Open **Erasing**. Top to bottom: the intro, a silver line about Blizzard's item restoration service, then **What Counts as Junk** with its intro and six checkboxes, two to a line: **Completed Quest Items** and **Dead-End Quest Starters**, **Outgrown Food & Drink** and **Outgrown Arrows & Bullets**, **White Weapons & Armor** and **Gray Trash**. Hover any box: its tooltip must end `Unchecked, it isn't junk: never erased, sold or pulled from the bank.` Below come **Maximum Value to Erase**, **Manual Delete Assistance**, and last **Erase Confirmation**, reading `Asks Yes or No before anything is erased.` above **Enable Erase Confirmation**; on every flavor where you did not run step 1, that switch must start **off**. With a gray named under **Lowest-Value Item**, uncheck **Gray Trash**: without closing the window, the mini-map button's icon must change, every gray must lose its tooltip line, and none may be named. Uncheck the other five: a red line must read `No kind is checked, so only your Erase List is erased or sold.` Check all six again, and the red line must go. Failure is a box on a line of its own, an unchecked kind still flagged, the old **Erase / Erase, Ask First / Keep** dropdowns anywhere, or the red line showing while any box is checked.

**3.** Turn **Enable Erase Confirmation** on, close the window, and Left-Click the mini-map button with your stack of grays named: a dialog must ask `Erase [item] x3?` (with your stack's count) and offer **Yes** and **No**. Click **No**: the stack stays and chat stays silent. Left-Click again and click **Yes**: the stack goes, and chat prints exactly `Erased [item] x3.`, with no value and no reason. Now Right-Click to protect whatever is named until the button names your *Fish Oil*, which is on your Erase List: a Left-Click must ask first for it too. Click **No**. Open **Your Current Bags**: the top row's reason must read **Erase List**, never **Asks First**, and its **Erase** button must bring up the same dialog. Click **No**. Middle-Click the button to clear the Protect List, turn **Enable Erase Confirmation** off, and Left-Click with a gray named: it must go without asking. Failure is an erase with no dialog while the switch is on, a dialog while it is off, the Erase List item skipping the dialog, an item gone after **No**, or a price or reason in the chat line.

**4.** Open the General page. Under **Features** must be eight switches, two to a line, in this order: **Enable Auto-Vend** and **Enable Bank Retrieval**, **Enable Maximum Value to Erase** and **Enable Manual Delete Assistance**, **Enable Erase Confirmation** and **Enable Tooltip Warnings**, **Enable Quest Item Alerts** and **Enable Bag-Space Warnings**. Turn **Enable Erase Confirmation** on here, open **Erasing**, and its own copy must be on; turn it off there, and the General copy must match. Then on **Erasing**, turn **Enable Maximum Value to Erase** on, pick **1 Gold**, and turn it off again. With your junk stack worth more than a gold in your bags, hover the mini-map button and note the **Clutter Report**. Back on the General page, turn **Enable Maximum Value to Erase** on, and without touching your bags hover the button again: the Clutter Report must already have dropped by that stack's bag slot and value. Leave the cap on for step 21. Failure is a switch missing, out of order or on a line of its own, two copies of a switch disagreeing, or a Clutter Report that only catches up after your bags change.

### Every class's reagents on the Erase List

**5.** On each flavor, make a brand-new character of any class but Shaman and log in once. Open **Erase List**: on Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and WoW Forever, its own pane must already list the reagents of every other class, among them *Arcane Powder*, *Soul Shard*, *Holy Candle*, *Symbol of Divinity*, *Flash Powder*, *Wild Berries*, *Ankh*, *Fish Oil* and *Shiny Fish Scales*, and none of its own class's (a Mage has no *Arcane Powder*, a Warlock no *Soul Shard*, a Rogue no *Flash Powder*). On Season of Discovery, whose data lists only the Shaman's reagents, the pane must hold just *Shiny Fish Scales* and *Fish Oil*. Then on your main character, who played the last release, the pane must still hold only what it had before the update. Click **Restore Defaults** and confirm: it must fill with the same full set, less its own class's. Failure is a reagent of the character's own class on its list, a class missing, a character from the last release topped up without Restore Defaults, or a Lua error.

### Fixes on the older clients

**6.** On **TBC Anniversary**, hover a gray in your bags: its tooltip must carry the red `Magic Eraser // May be erased.` line, and an item you protect with a Right-Click must carry the white `Magic Eraser // On your Protect List.` line. On **Classic Era** and **TBC Anniversary**, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe* must carry the same red line, while your white profession tool and white shirt carry none. On **WoW Forever**, the mini-map button's icon must sit centered and round inside its ring, like the other buttons around the mini-map. Failure is no Magic Eraser line at all on TBC Anniversary, a white weapon or armor piece never flagged on Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, or a square or off-center icon on WoW Forever.

**7.** On **Classic Era** and **TBC Anniversary**, turn **Enable Bank Retrieval** off, put one spare gray in the bag in your **first** bank bag slot, and close the bank. Hover the mini-map button and note the **Clutter Report**. Open the bank again: hovering that gray inside the bank must show no Magic Eraser line, the button must never name it, and the Clutter Report must read the same as before. Close the bank, turn Bank Retrieval back on, and open it once more: the gray must come home with `Pulled 1 item (1 bag slot) out of your bank, worth X.` Failure is the bank gray marked, named or counted while the bank is open, or a Left-Click reaching into the bank.

### Bag-Space Warnings after being switched off

**8.** On **Alerts & Tooltips**, turn **Enable Bag-Space Warnings** on, set **Free-Slot Threshold** to 10, then turn the warnings off. Fill your bags until 10 or fewer slots are free; splitting stacks works. Turn the warnings back on, close the window, and move an item from one bag slot to an empty one: chat must stay silent. Split one more stack: exactly one line must read `Your bags are nearly full. You have N slots remaining.` with your new count. Set the threshold back to 4. Failure is a warning on the move, when no slot was lost, or none on the split.

### Diagnostic Tools in tabs

**9.** Open **Diagnostic Tools**. With **Enable Diagnostic Tools** off, only the warning paragraph and the toggle may show, with no empty bordered box beneath. Turn it on: four tabs must appear, **Run Tests**, **Settings**, **Code** and **Data**. **Run Tests** must hold **Event Log**, **Taint Log**, **In-Game Tools** with copy boxes for `/etrace` and `/console scriptErrors 1`, and **External Tools** with copy boxes holding the CurseForge addresses of Bug Grabber and Bug Sack. **Settings** must hold a **Run All Settings Reports** button over four rows, **Eraser Context**, **Saved Variables**, **Display Context** and **Other Add-ons**, each with a **Run** button on the right. Click **Run** on **Eraser Context**: the row must read **Done**, and the box's first line must end `// Flavor Vanilla // Data Vanilla` on Classic Era, `// Flavor Camelot // Data Camelot` on WoW Forever, `// Flavor TBC // Data TBC` on TBC Anniversary, and `// Flavor Vanilla (Season of Discovery) // Data Discovery` on Season of Discovery, with a `---- Eraser Context ----` line below it. Click **Run All Settings Reports**: the box must hold that first line once, then one `---- ... ----` block per report in row order. Turn the toggle off and on again: every row's state and the box must be cleared. Failure is an empty box with the toggle off, a missing tab or row, the wrong flavor or data folder, a second header line, or a Lua error.

**10.** Open **Data**. There must be six rows, **Quest-Items**, **Quest-Starting-Items**, **Consumables**, **Ammo**, **Equipment** and **Class-Reagents**, each naming its file underneath (`Vanilla/Ammo-Vanilla.lua` on Classic Era, `Discovery/Ammo-Discovery.lua` on Season of Discovery, `TBC/Ammo-TBC.lua` on TBC Anniversary, `Camelot/Ammo-Camelot.lua` on WoW Forever) beside a **Validate** button. Click **Validate All Data Files**: every run button must gray out, and the rows must go `Waiting`, `Running` with a climbing `N / M IDs`, then `Done` with their STATUS counts, one file at a time and in order. The box must hold the client header line once, then one `---- Validate Data: ... ----` block per file. No row may read `TABLE MISSING`, and in the Class-Reagents block every row must read `OK` with a class under `DATA_CLASS`; note any `NOT ON CLIENT` or `NO TITLE` count for your report, which is data for the next release rather than this step failing. Click it again and press **Stop** partway: the run must end at once, the remaining rows must read `Stopped`, the box must end with `(stopped before finishing)`, and the buttons must come back. Failure is a file named for the wrong folder, a file out of order, a second header line, a button usable mid-run, a run that keeps going after Stop, or a Lua error. **Season of Discovery is the one to watch**: it runs on the Classic Era client, so only these file names show it picked up its own data.

When steps 1 to 10 pass on every flavor, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**11.** Log in with the add-on freshly enabled. Exactly one branded line must print, naming a version and pointing at Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser. On an unpackaged development copy the version reads `Dev`, which is correct; a packaged release must read a real version number on every flavor. Then `/reload`. Failure is no message, a doubled message, a literal `%s` where the version belongs, or any Lua error on either load.

**12.** Reach the Options Interface all three ways. Type `/eraser`. Close it and hold Shift while Middle-Clicking the mini-map button. Close it again, open the Blizzard Options window yourself and pick **Magic Eraser** out of the category list. All three must land on the same page, **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, and under the **/Commands** header `/eraser` must be listed in blue followed by **Opens the Options Interface for this add-on.** Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a run on Classic Era alone has not finished this step.

**13.** Get into combat and type `/eraser`. One line must read `Magic Eraser // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat.` and the panel must **not** open. Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button while still in combat: same line, same refusal. Ask three or four more times each way, and the line must print **every** time. Still in combat, with junk in your bags, Left-Click the button: chat must print `Magic Eraser // Cannot erase items while in combat.` and nothing may be erased. Then let combat end without touching anything: the panel must not open by itself and nothing may be erased late. Failure is the panel opening, an item vanishing, silence after the first refusal, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Magic Eraser.

**14.** Hover the mini-map button with several flagged items of different values in your bags. The tooltip must read, top to bottom: the add-on name with the version on the right; **Lowest-Value Item** with the item's icon, link and value; **Left-Click / Erase**; **Right-Click / Protect**; **Auto-Vend** showing Enabled or Disabled, with **Shift + Right-Click / Toggle**; **Clutter Report** with a bag-slot count, an item count and a total; the **Protect List** with **Middle-Click / Clear Protect List**, if you have anything protected; and last, **Magic Eraser Options** with **Shift + Middle-Click**. The button's icon must match the named item, and that item must be the cheapest **whole stack**: twenty grays at 1 copper are worth 20 copper and come after a single 5 copper item. Now work the clicks. Left-Click erases the named item, plays a sound, prints `Erased [item].` with a working item link (and ` x5` after it for a stack of five), and refreshes the tooltip **while you are still hovering**. Right-Click adds the named item to this character's Protect List block. Middle-Click empties that block. Shift + Right-Click flips Auto-Vend between Enabled and Disabled live, and **Enable Auto-Vend** on the General page must match; leave it Enabled. Failure is a block missing or out of order, a dearer stack offered first, a click doing something other than its label, or a tooltip that catches up only after you move the mouse away and back.

**15.** On the General page, under **Key Bindings**, three rows must sit in blue: **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, **Add Hovered Item to Protect List** and **Add Hovered Item to Erase List**, each followed by its key or a gray **Not Bound** and a **Set Key** button. Click **Set Key**: the game's key binding list must open, holding a **Magic Eraser** section with exactly those three entries. Bind your three spare keys, come back, and each row must show its key without a reload. Hover the mini-map button, note the named item, move the mouse away, and press the erase key: that item must go exactly as a Left-Click erases it, with the same chat line. Turn **Enable Erase Confirmation** on, press the erase key, and the `Erase [item]?` dialog must appear; click **No** and turn the switch back off. Point at empty ground and press either list key: chat must print `Magic Eraser // Hover over an item, then press the key again.` Hover *Linen Cloth* in your bags and press the Erase List key: chat must print `Added [Linen Cloth] to your Erase List.` with a working item link, and **without moving the mouse** its tooltip must gain the red `Magic Eraser // On your Erase List, and may be erased.` line within a moment. Press the Protect List key: `Moved [Linen Cloth] from your Erase List to your Protect List.`, and the line must turn to a white `Magic Eraser // On your Protect List.` Press the Erase List key again: `[Linen Cloth] is on your Protect List, which always wins. Remove it from there first.` Then remove it from the Protect List, since step 19 needs it on neither list. Failure is a missing row, a raw name such as `MAGICERASER_ERASE`, a **Set Key** that opens nothing, a different item going than the one named, a key press erasing past the dialog, a missing or doubled chat line, or a tooltip that catches up only after you move the mouse away and back. If **Set Key** prints `Open the game menu, then Options, then Key Bindings, and find the Magic Eraser section.` instead, note the flavor and paste the three Set Key rows from step 24's API report. **WoW Forever reads the hovered item a different way from the other clients**, so a pass on one flavor proves nothing about the others.

**16.** Check the outgrown boundaries, on characters at the right levels or one you can level: *Refreshing Spring Water* is flagged from level 5, *Ice Cold Milk* from 15 and *Melon Juice* from 25; *Rough Arrow* from 10, *Sharp Arrow* from 25 and *Razor Arrow* from 40; and none of them one level earlier. *Jagged Arrow* must never be flagged on Classic Era, Season of Discovery or WoW Forever, at any level, and on TBC Anniversary only from level 55. Then carry a drink one level short of qualifying and **level up**: the candidate must update on the level-up itself, with you touching nothing. Failure is a boundary landing somewhere else, a Jagged Arrow flagged where nothing better is sold, or having to move an item before the add-on notices.

**17.** Pick a quest chain that hands you an item a later quest still needs; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar are the quickest. Work it up to the quest **before** the last one that needs the item, turn that one in, and accept the next so the item is back in your bags. Stop there and wait a few seconds: chat must print **nothing** about the item, its tooltip must carry **no** Magic Eraser line, and it must never be named under **Lowest-Value Item**. Type `/reload` and check all three again. Now turn in the last quest that needs it. If the item is still in your bags, within about a second chat must print exactly one line reading `[item] can now be safely erased. It's left over from a quest you have completed.` (with ` x2` after the link for a stack of two), its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // May be erased.`, and it must become the candidate; Left-Click must print `Erased [item].` If the turn-in used the item up, silence is correct. Failure before that last turn-in is the dangerous one: it means the add-on is offering to erase a quest item the character is about to need.

**18.** Put a spent quest starter in your bank, `/reload`, and open the bank so Bank Retrieval hands it back; looting a fresh one works too. Within about a second of it landing in your bags, chat must print exactly one line: `[item] can now be safely erased. It's left over from a quest you have completed.` when the quest is already done, or `[item] can now be safely erased. It starts a quest your character can't take.` when your race or class is locked out. Move it between bag slots, then close and reopen the bank: it must **not** print again. Turn **Enable Quest Item Alerts** off, put it back in the bank, `/reload` and reopen the bank: no line at all, but it must still be named under **Lowest-Value Item** once it is the cheapest. Turn the alerts back on. Left-Click it: chat must read `Erased [item].` Separately, on a character who **can** take that quest and has not done it, carry a copy: no alert, no tooltip line, never the candidate. Failure is silence with the alerts on, a line with them off, a repeat on every bag change, the wrong sentence for the reason, or, worst of all, the eligible character being offered a quest starter it still needs.

**19.** Confirm what is never touched, one item at a time: an item your client draws in **green**, white trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*, your equipped gear, a quest item for a quest you have not finished, and a gray a vendor will not buy. None may show a Magic Eraser line and none may ever be named under **Lowest-Value Item**; keep the bag items with you for step 21, where none may sell. Pick a trade good that is **not** on your Erase List, since the seeded fishing reagents are white trade goods the add-on is supposed to take. Run the green half on **every** flavor with the same item, because a handful of low-level crafted pieces are white on one client and green on another, and the client in front of you decides. Failure here is the most dangerous kind of bug this add-on can have.

**20.** On your main character, open **Erase List**. That character's own pane must list **Shiny Fish Scales** and **Fish Oil**. Carry your *Fish Oil* stack: its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // On your Erase List, and may be erased.` in red. Remove Shiny Fish Scales with its remove icon: it must go with one click and no confirmation, and must not come back after a `/reload`. Log in on a Shaman and check the same pane: it must list **no** Shaman reagent, neither *Fish Oil*, *Shiny Fish Scales* nor *Ankh*. Failure is a Fish Oil stack with no red line, a removed row coming back after a reload, or a Shaman starting with their own reagents listed.

**21.** Take your bags to a merchant with **Enable Auto-Vend** on and its chat dropdown set to **Every Sale in Chat**. Everything flagged that a vendor will buy must sell on opening, including the junk stack worth more than a gold with step 4's cap still on. Each sale prints one line reading `Sold [item], worth [price].` with a working, quality-colored item link you can click, and closing the merchant window prints `Sold N items (M bag slots), worth X.` Left in your bags must be everything from step 19's never-touched set, your white profession tool and shirt, and anything on your Protect List. Turn the cap off. Switch the dropdown to **Summary in Chat**, buy one gray back from **Buyback**, close the window, and talk to the merchant again: on closing, only `Sold 1 item (1 bag slot), worth X.` may print. Failure is a broken or half-rendered link, a stray `%s`, a line that is not one complete sentence, "1 items" or "1 bag slots", a per-item line under Summary in Chat, the capped stack left unsold, or anything protected or never-touched sold.

**22.** Put your two spare grays in your bank, one in its own slots and one in the bag sitting in your first bank bag slot, and close it. Open it again: both must come back under one line, `Pulled 2 items (2 bag slots) out of your bank, worth X.` Then put more flagged items in your bank than you have free slots, turn **Enable Bag-Space Warnings** on with **Free-Slot Threshold** at 4, and open the bank: retrieval must stop with at least four slots still free. Failure is a gray left behind, no line in chat, or a bank that ignores the threshold while the warnings are on. **WoW Forever is the flavor to watch**, since its bank is the one most likely to be laid out differently.

**23.** Open **Erasing** and note the word the **Manual Delete Assistance** paragraph quotes. With **Enable Manual Delete Assistance** on and the dropdown beside it on **Items with No Sale Value**, drag your sellable Rare item out of your bags and drop it on the ground: the game's own prompt must appear untouched, asking you to type that same word, letter for letter. Press **No**. Switch the dropdown to **All Items** and drop the item again: the prompt must now be a plain Yes or No, with no typing box and a **Yes** button you can click. Press **No**, and the item must still be in your bags. Set the dropdown back to **Items with No Sale Value**. Failure is a different word than the game's prompt, the typing box surviving under All Items, a grayed-out Yes, or the item gone after No. **WoW Forever is the flavor where this is least proven.**

**24.** `/reload` and open **Diagnostic Tools**. **Enable Diagnostic Tools** must be **off**, with only the warning paragraph and that toggle visible. Turn it on, open **Code**, and click **Run** on **API Endpoints**. Note every `[FAIL]` row on each flavor for your report; a FAIL alone is not this step failing, because several rows are alternatives where one half is enough. This step fails if any of these reads FAIL: a `C_Container` row, `C_Item.GetItemInfo`, `C_Item.GetItemInfoInstant`, `C_Item.GetItemSpell`, `DELETE_GOOD_ITEM`, `DELETE_ITEM_CONFIRM_STRING` or any of the four `StaticPopup1` rows (step 23 depends on them). It also fails if **both** halves of a pair read FAIL: `C_Item.GetItemStats` with `GetItemStats (legacy)` (white gear is never erased without one), `GameTooltip.ProcessInfo (data-driven tooltips)` with `GameTooltip.SetBagItem (legacy)`, `C_TooltipInfo.GetItemByID` with `Hidden scan tooltip (legacy)`, `TooltipUtil.GetDisplayedItem` with `GameTooltip.GetItem (legacy)`, or `C_QuestLog.GetTitleForQuestID` with `C_QuestLog.GetQuestInfo`; or if all three Set Key routes, `Settings.KEYBINDINGS_CATEGORY_ID`, `SettingsPanel.GetCategoryList` and `KeyBindingFrame_LoadUI (legacy)`, read FAIL. Then `/reload` and reopen the panel: the toggle must be back **off**. Failure is also the toggle surviving a reload, or a Lua error from the button.

**25.** Optional, on a non-English client: open the Options Interface, walk every Magic Eraser page, find the **Magic Eraser** section in the game's key binding list, hover the mini-map button, and erase and sell one item. Every string must render as real words in that language, with no raw keys such as `TAB_ERASING` showing through, and an unbound key's **Not Bound** must read in the client's own words. Strings new in this release, the junk-kind tooltips and Erase Confirmation among them, may still show in English until the Localization pass, which is expected, and every number and item name must land inside its sentence rather than leaving a bare `%s` or `%d` behind. The **Manual Delete Assistance** paragraph must quote the same word that client's own delete prompt asks for, and the closing line for selling one item must read naturally for a single item. Failure is a raw key, a stray placeholder, a sentence assembled in the wrong order, or a quoted word the client's own prompt does not use.

When every step passes on Classic Era, WoW Forever, TBC Anniversary and Season of Discovery, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
