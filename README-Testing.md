# Magic Eraser // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Magic Eraser, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on Classic Era, then `/reload` and run it again on TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these first, so you are not caught short halfway through:

- **Characters.** One character at level 15 or higher on each flavor, who is **not** a Shaman. A second character on the same account for the Global list check in step 19, and a Shaman on the same account for step 20. For step 16 you want a character partway through one of the quest chains named there; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore for Alliance and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar for Horde both open before level 10. For step 17 you want a character who is locked out of a quest by race or class, and one who is not: any non-Human for *Goldshire Gift Voucher*, any non-Tauren for *Bloodhoof Village Gift Voucher*, any non-Rogue for *Elegant Letter*.
- **A spare key.** One key you use for nothing else, to bind in step 2. Clear the binding when you finish, since a stray press erases.
- **Items and bags.** Two spare grays, each a single item rather than a stack and each worth less than your Fish Oil stack, for steps 3 to 7. More gray trash with a sell price. A curated white, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*. An item your client draws in green. White trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*. *Refreshing Spring Water*, *Ice Cold Milk* and *Melon Juice*. A single stack of junk worth more than one gold. A stack of *Fish Oil*. A bound item that starts a quest and is already spent, meaning either the quest is done or your race or class can never take it. A quest item for a quest you have not finished. For step 8, a **Rare (blue) item** a vendor will buy; that step only opens the game's delete prompt and cancels it. Keep a few free bag slots.
- **Park your fixtures before step 1.** Steps 3 to 6 erase and sell flagged items, meaning anything whose tooltip reads `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.` or `Magic Eraser // Flagged by your Erase List.` Open your bank and put every flagged item in it except the two spare grays and the Fish Oil stack; step 7 hands the rest back. Opening the bank may pull a few flagged items out first, so park those too.
- **Location and state.** Out of combat except where a step says otherwise. A merchant and a bank you can reach, ideally close together, and something willing to fight you for steps 4 and 11.
- **A non-English client**, for the optional last step only.

Every step below has a pass or fail you can see. To stay short enough to finish in one sitting, this plan deliberately skips the erase confirmation dialog beyond the one check in step 4, the welcome and mini-map toggles, profile switching, adding items by ID inside either list panel, Restore Defaults on the Erase List, the Bag-Space Warnings countdown, Auto-Vend deferring a sale until combat ends, and the Validate Data reports.

## Verify this release's changes

Everything in this section is new since 2026.09.07.A, the last packaged build, so this is the part worth running carefully.

### Erase key binding

**1.** Type `/eraser` and read the General page top to bottom. It must run: the add-on's description, **Enable Welcome Message**, **Enable Mini-map Button**, the **/Commands** header with `/eraser` beneath it, then a new **Key Bindings** header with **Erase Lowest-Value Item** in blue, followed by a line saying it does exactly what Left-clicking the mini-map button does, that you use it at your own risk, and where to set it. Below that comes **Auto-Vend** with its toggle and the indented **Enable Auto-Vend Messages** row beside its dropdown, then **Feedback & Support** and the muted version line. Failure is the Key Bindings section missing or sitting anywhere else, a raw key such as `OPTIONS_KEY_BINDINGS_HEADER` showing through, or any Auto-Vend control lost or out of place, since that section moved down to make room.

**2.** Follow those directions: open **Key Bindings** from the game menu and find the **Magic Eraser** section. It must hold exactly one entry, **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, named exactly as the General page names it. Bind your spare key to it, accept the change, and `/reload`: the key must still be bound when you look again. Failure is no Magic Eraser section, the entry filed under some other heading or showing a raw name such as `MAGICERASER_ERASE`, a name that differs from the General page, directions that do not lead you there, or the binding gone after the reload.

**3.** Hover the mini-map button and note the item under **Lowest-Value Item**, which should be one of your spare grays. Move the mouse away and press your key once. That item must go exactly as a Left-Click erases it: the erase sound, one chat line reading `Erased [item], worth [price].` with a working item link, and the button's icon moving on to the next item. Failure is nothing happening, a Lua error, a different item going than the tooltip named, a second item going on the same press, or the chat line printing while the item is still on your cursor or in your bags.

**4.** Check that a key press stops wherever a click would. Get into combat and press the key: one line must read `Magic Eraser // Cannot erase items while in combat.` and nothing may go, then or after the fight ends. Out of combat, with your second spare gray named on the button, open **Safety Features**, turn on **Mini-map Eraser Confirmation** with **For Gray Vendor-Trash Items** ticked, close the Options window, and press the key: the same `Erase [item]?` dialog a Left-Click raises must appear, naming that gray. Click **No**, and the gray must still be in your bags. Turn **Mini-map Eraser Confirmation** back off. Failure is a key press erasing anything a Left-Click would refuse or ask about first, the worst failure in this section, because a key shows you nothing before it acts.

### One item, one bag slot

**5.** Take your remaining spare gray and your Fish Oil stack to a merchant, with Auto-Vend on and **Summary Only** selected, the shipped defaults. Opening the merchant sells both, and a moment after you close the window one line must read `Sold N items (M bag slots), worth X.` Buy the gray back from the merchant's **Buyback** tab, close the window (nothing prints, correctly, since nothing sold), and talk to the merchant again: on closing, the line must read `Sold 1 item (1 bag slot), worth X.` Do the same with the stack on its own: `Sold N items (1 bag slot), worth X.` Failure is "1 items" or "1 bag slots", a sale with no closing line, or a line that is not one complete sentence.

**6.** Still at the merchant, buy the gray back and hover the mini-map button, with the gray the only flagged item in your bags: the **Clutter Report** must read **1 Bag Slot (1 Item)** beside the gray's value. Buy the stack back as well: it must now read **2 Bag Slots**, with the count in brackets covering the whole stack plus the gray. Failure is "1 Bag Slots", "(1 Items)", or a count that does not match what you carry.

**7.** Take both to your bank. Opening it hands back everything you parked before step 1, under one line such as `Pulled N items (M bag slots) out of your bank, worth X.` If your spent quest starter comes back with them, its alert line printing too is correct; put it back in the bank for step 17. Now put the gray in the bank on its own, close the bank, and open it again: the line must read `Pulled 1 item (1 bag slot) out of your bank, worth X.` Do the same with the stack on its own: `Pulled N items (1 bag slot) out of your bank, worth X.` Failure is the same as step 5, or anything flagged left behind in the bank.

### Safety Features wording

**8.** Open **Safety Features** and read the **Manual Delete Assistance** paragraph. The word in quotes must be the word the game's own delete prompt asks you to type, letter for letter and in the same capitals: `DELETE` on an English client. To compare, leave **Applies To** on the shipped **No Sale Value**, drag your Rare item out of your bags and drop it on the ground, read the prompt, and press **Cancel**. While you are on that page, the **Bag-Space Warnings** paragraph must now say the add-on counts down in chat **once** your free bag slots drop to the threshold. Failure is a different word or different capitals than the game's prompt, empty quotes, a bare `%s`, or the Bag-Space paragraph still reading "as your free bag slots drop".

When steps 1 to 8 pass on both flavors, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**9.** Log in with the add-on freshly enabled. Exactly one branded line must print, naming a version and pointing at Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser. On an unpackaged development copy the version reads `Dev`, which is correct; a packaged release must read a real version number. Then `/reload`. Failure is no message, a doubled message, a literal `%s` where the version belongs, or any Lua error on either load.

**10.** Reach the Options Interface all three ways. Type `/eraser`. Close it and hold Shift while Middle-Clicking the mini-map button. Close it again, open the Blizzard Options window yourself and pick **Magic Eraser** out of the category list. All three must land on the same docked page, and under the **/Commands** header `/eraser` must be listed in blue followed by **Opens the Options Interface for this add-on.** Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a run on Classic Era alone has not finished this step.

**11.** Get into combat and type `/eraser`. One line must read `Magic Eraser // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat.` and the panel must **not** open. Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button while still in combat: same line, same refusal. Ask three or four more times each way, and the line must print **every** time. Still in combat, Left-Click the button with junk in your bags: one line must read `Magic Eraser // Cannot erase items while in combat.` and nothing may be erased. Then let combat end without touching anything: the panel must not open by itself and nothing may be erased late. Failure is the panel opening, an item vanishing, silence after the first refusal, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Magic Eraser.

**12.** Hover the mini-map button with junk in your bags. The tooltip must read, top to bottom: the add-on name with the version on the right; **Lowest-Value Item** with the item's icon, link and value; **Left-Click / Erase**; **Right-Click / Ignore**; **Auto-Vend** showing Enabled or Disabled, with its description and **Shift + Right-Click / Toggle**; **Clutter Report** with a bag-slot count, an item count and a total; the **Ignore List** with **Middle-Click / Clear Ignore List** below it, if you have anything ignored; and last, **Magic Eraser Options** with **Shift + Middle-Click**. The button's icon must match the item named under Lowest-Value Item. Failure is a missing block, a block out of that order, an empty line where a value belongs, or an icon that does not match.

**13.** Work through the clicks and confirm each does what the tooltip beside it says. Left-Click erases the named item, plays a sound, prints `Erased [item], worth [price].` with a working item link, and refreshes the tooltip to the next item **while you are still hovering**. Right-Click adds the named item to this character's Ignore List, and it appears in the tooltip's Ignore List block. Middle-Click empties that block. Shift + Right-Click flips the Auto-Vend line between Enabled and Disabled live, and **Enable Auto-Vend** on the General page must be flipped to match. Failure is any click doing something other than its label, the two Auto-Vend readings disagreeing, a broken link in the erase line, or a tooltip that catches up only after you move the mouse away and back.

**14.** With several flagged items of different values in your bags, confirm the candidate really is the cheapest, comparing whole stacks rather than single items: twenty grays at 1 copper is worth 20 copper and outranks a single 5 copper item. Then make two flagged items worth the same and check the tie-break, which runs Erase List items first, then completed quest items, then gray trash, then consumables and curated whites together. Failure is a more valuable stack being offered first, or a tie broken the other way.

**15.** Check the outgrown-consumable boundary, on one character you can level or on three at the right levels: *Refreshing Spring Water* is flagged from level 5, *Ice Cold Milk* from 15, *Melon Juice* from 25, and none of the three one level below that. Then carry a drink one level short of qualifying and **level up**: the candidate must update on the level-up itself, with you touching nothing. Failure is a boundary landing somewhere else, or having to move an item before the add-on notices.

**16.** Pick a quest chain that hands you an item a later quest still needs; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar are the quickest. Work it up to the quest **before** the last one that needs the item, turn that one in, and accept the next so the item is back in your bags, since several of these chains take the item at one turn-in and hand it straight back for the next. Stop there and wait a few seconds: chat must print **nothing** about the item, its tooltip must carry **no** Magic Eraser line, and it must never be named under **Lowest-Value Item** or wear its icon on the button. Type `/reload` and check all three again. Now turn in the last quest that needs it. If the item is still in your bags, within about a second chat must print exactly one line reading `[item] can now be safely erased!`, its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.`, and it must become the candidate; Left-Click must print `Erased [item], left over from a quest you have completed.` If the turn-in used the item up, silence is correct. Failure before that last turn-in is the dangerous one: it means the add-on is offering to erase a quest item the character is about to need.

**17.** Put a spent quest starter in your bank, `/reload`, and open the bank so Bank Retrieval hands it back; looting a fresh one works too. Within about a second of it landing in your bags, chat must print exactly one line: `[item] can now be safely erased!` when the quest is already complete, or `[item] can be safely erased. It starts a quest your character cannot take.` when your race or class is locked out. Move it between bag slots, then open and close a merchant: it must **not** print again. Hover the mini-map button and Left-Click once it is the named candidate: chat must read `Erased [item], which starts a quest your character cannot take.` for a lock-out, and `Erased [item], left over from a quest you have completed.` for a quest you finished. Now carry that same race-locked or class-locked item on a character who **can** take the quest and has not done it: no alert, no tooltip line, never the candidate. Failure is silence, a repeat line on every bag change, the wrong sentence for the reason, or, worst of all, the eligible character being offered a quest starter it still needs.

**18.** Confirm what is flagged and what never is. Put a curated white in your bags, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*: its name must be white, and its tooltip must carry a red `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.` line. Then work through the never-touched set one item at a time: an item your client draws in **green**, white trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*, your equipped gear, a quest item for a quest you have not finished, and a gray a vendor will not buy. None may be flagged, none may show a Magic Eraser line, and none may sell at a merchant. Pick a trade good that is **not** on your Erase List, since the seeded fishing reagents are white trade goods the add-on is supposed to take. Run the green half on **both** flavors with the same item, because a handful of low-level crafted pieces are white on one client and green on the other, and the client in front of you decides. Failure here is the most dangerous kind of bug this add-on can have.

**19.** Right-Click the mini-map button to ignore the current candidate, then confirm it is skipped everywhere: its tooltip reads `Magic Eraser // Protected by your Ignore List.` in white, it never becomes the candidate, Auto-Vend leaves it alone at a merchant, and Bank Retrieval leaves it in the bank. Open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Ignore List**, find that row under this character and click its **Global** button: the row must leave this character's pane and appear under **Global**. Log in on a second character with its own Ignore List empty: the same item must still be protected everywhere. Failure is a protected item being erased, sold or retrieved on either character, or the promoted row showing up in both panes.

**20.** Log in on a character that is **not** a Shaman and open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Erase List**. That character's own scope must already list **Shiny Fish Scales** and **Fish Oil**, each drawn as a real item link with its icon rather than a raw ID. Click the **Global** button on Shiny Fish Scales: that row must leave this pane and turn up under **Global**. Remove it there with its remove icon: it must go with one click and no confirmation, and must not come back to either pane after a `/reload`. Log in on a Shaman and check the same pane: it must list **neither** reagent. Failure is an empty list on a fresh non-Shaman, a promoted row showing in both panes, a removed row coming back after a reload, or a Shaman starting with their own reagents listed.

**21.** With *Fish Oil* still on that non-Shaman's Erase List, carry a stack. Its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // Flagged by your Erase List.` in red, it can become the candidate, it sells at a merchant, and it walks out of the bank. Turn on **Maximum Value to Erase** with the limit set below that stack's total value: the listed item must **still** be flagged, because the cap does not apply to a list you built. Last, add the same item to this character's **Ignore List** as well: the tooltip must flip to `Magic Eraser // Protected by your Ignore List.` and the item must be spared everywhere, even though it now sits on both lists. Failure is the cap suppressing a listed item, or the Erase List winning over the Ignore List.

**22.** Open **Safety Features** and find **Maximum Value to Erase**. Turn it on and set the dropdown, which must read **1 Gold, 2 Gold, 3 Gold, 5 Gold, 8 Gold, 13 Gold, 21 Gold** in that order, to **1 Gold**, with a stack of junk in your bags worth more than a gold. Without reloading, the mini-map icon and **Lowest-Value Item** must move to something cheaper, the **Clutter Report** must drop by that stack, and the stack's tooltip must show **no** Magic Eraser line. Then take the stack to a merchant with Auto-Vend on: it must still sell, and a second one in your bank must still walk back into your bags. Failure is "13 Gold" sitting between "1 Gold" and "2 Gold", the button still wearing that stack's icon, or the cap blocking a sale or a retrieval.

**23.** Sell a batch at a merchant with **Enable Auto-Vend Messages** on and the mode set to **Line Item**. Each sale prints one line reading `Sold [item], worth [price].` with a working, quality-colored item link you can click, and closing the merchant window prints one more line reading `Sold N items (M bag slots), worth X.` Switch to **Summary Only**, the shipped default, and sell another batch: only the closing line prints. Then open a bank holding several flagged items: one line must read `Pulled N items (M bag slots) out of your bank, worth X.` On a fresh profile, with Bag-Space Warnings off, retrieval may use every free slot you have. Turn **Enable Bag-Space Warnings** on with **Free-Slot Threshold** at 4 and open the bank again: this time retrieval must stop with at least four slots still free. Every one of these lines must read as a complete sentence. Failure is a broken or half-rendered link, a stray `%s`, a per-item line in Summary Only mode, or a bank that ignores the threshold once the warnings are on.

**24.** Open **Diagnostic Tools**. On a fresh login **Enable Diagnostic Tools** must be **off**, with only the warning paragraph and that toggle visible. Turn it on and click **Test WoW API Endpoints**. Every line must read PASS, with one exception: the four modern and legacy pairs, the three `C_AddOns` pairs and the tooltip pair, where at least one half must pass and a FAIL on the other half is the report working rather than a defect. Note which half passed for each pair, on each flavor. `Settings.OpenToCategory` stands alone and must read PASS, `InterfaceOptionsFrame_OpenToCategory` must not appear anywhere in the list, and the seven Manual Delete Assistance rows, `StaticPopup_FindVisible`, `DELETE_GOOD_ITEM`, `DELETE_ITEM_CONFIRM_STRING` and the four `StaticPopup1` methods, must all read PASS. Then `/reload` and reopen the panel: the toggle must be back **off**. Failure is the toggle surviving a reload, both halves of any pair reading FAIL, or a FAIL on any line outside those pairs.

**25.** Optional, on a non-English client: open the Options Interface, walk the General and Safety Features pages, find the **Magic Eraser** section in the game's Key Bindings list, hover the mini-map button, and erase and sell one item. Every string must render as real words in that language, with no raw keys such as `TAB_SAFETY` showing through, and every number and item name must land inside its sentence rather than leaving a bare `%s` or `%d` behind. The **Manual Delete Assistance** paragraph must quote the same word that client's own delete prompt asks for, and the closing line for selling one item must read naturally for a single item. Failure is a raw key, a stray placeholder, a sentence assembled in the wrong order, or a quoted word the client's own prompt does not use.

When every step passes on both Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
