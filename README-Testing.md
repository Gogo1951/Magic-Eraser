# Magic Eraser // Test Plan

Magic Eraser spots the junk in your bags and erases it a click at a time, or sells it at your next merchant. This time we most want you at the bank with a bag add-on such as Baganator, and on the new quest items for Season of Discovery and WoW Forever.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Open your bank through a bag add-on such as Baganator: Bank Retrieval brings your junk home, and no bags-full warning pops up until you close it.
2. On WoW Forever, finish a quest that leaves spares behind, such as Intact Limbs or Stolen Ore: chat tells you they're now safe to erase.
3. On Season of Discovery, loot a quest starter your class or faction can't take, such as Primitive Drawing on anyone but a Hunter: chat tells you it's safe to erase.
4. On WoW Forever, play a Skyborne and loot an item that starts a quest you can take: it stays.
5. Clear every bit of junk from your bags and hover the mini-map button: one congratulation, with no Clutter Report under it.
6. Find Skip Typing DELETE under Erasing (it used to be Delete Without Typing), then drag a Rare item no merchant will buy onto the ground: you get a plain Yes or No.

## Everything Else

7. Click the mini-map button to erase your Lowest-Value Item, then hover it and Protect the next one.
8. Open your bags and bank, then loot junk in combat on WoW Forever: only junk is tinted, even as it lands.
9. Open a merchant and let Auto-Vend sell your junk, then hold Shift on the next visit: nothing sells.
10. Turn in the last quest that needs an item still in your bags: chat tells you it's now safe to erase.
11. Bind **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, **Add Hovered Item to Protect List** and **Add Hovered Item to Erase List** under Key Bindings and try each one.
12. Type `/eraser`, then click Magic Eraser Options at the bottom of the menu: both open the options inside the game's Options window, and TBC Anniversary is where this breaks.
13. In combat, try `/eraser`, and Magic Eraser Options and Erase in the menu: all three refuse.
