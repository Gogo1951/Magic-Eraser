# Magic Eraser // Test Plan

Magic Eraser spots the junk in your bags and erases it a click at a time, or sells it at your next merchant. This time we most want you on gear you craft yourself and on skipping Auto-Vend for a visit.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Craft a white piece such as a Linen Cloak, then visit a merchant and your bank: it stays put until you tick Crafted Weapons & Armor under Erasing.
2. Hold Shift as you open a merchant: Auto-Vend sells nothing that visit.
3. On a new character, replace your starting boots and open a merchant: Auto-Vend sells the old pair.

## Everything Else

4. Hover the mini-map button to see your cheapest junk, then Left-Click to erase it or Right-Click to protect it.
5. Open your bags and bank, with your bag add-on if you use one: only the junk Magic Eraser would erase is tinted.
6. Loot junk in combat on WoW Forever: it's tinted as it lands.
7. Open a merchant and let Auto-Vend sell your junk: nothing green or better, no white trade goods such as Linen Cloth, and no quest item you still need ever sells.
8. Open your bank and let Bank Retrieval bring your junk home, watching WoW Forever most closely.
9. Turn in the last quest that needs an item still in your bags: chat tells you it's now safe to erase.
10. Bind **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, **Add Hovered Item to Protect List** and **Add Hovered Item to Erase List** under Key Bindings and try each one.
11. Drag a Rare item no vendor will buy onto the ground: you get a plain Yes or No instead of typing DELETE.
12. Type `/eraser`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button: both open the options inside the game's Options window, and TBC Anniversary is where this breaks.
13. In combat, try `/eraser`, Shift + Middle-Click and Left-Click on the mini-map button: all three refuse.
