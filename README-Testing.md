# Magic Eraser // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Magic Eraser, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on each flavor in turn: Classic Era, WoW Forever, TBC Anniversary, and Season of Discovery on the Classic Era client.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these first, so you are not caught short halfway through:

- **The last release, for step 1.** On one flavor, keep **2026.09.18.A** installed until step 1 tells you to update, on an account with at least two characters who have each logged in with it.
- **Characters.** On each flavor, one character at level 15 or higher who is **not** a Shaman. A second character on the same account for step 7, and a Shaman on the same account for step 19. For step 16 you want a character partway through one of the quest chains named there; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore for Alliance and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar for Horde both open before level 10. For step 17 you want a character who is locked out of a quest by race or class, and one who is not: any non-Human for *Goldshire Gift Voucher*, any non-Tauren for *Bloodhoof Village Gift Voucher*, any non-Rogue for *Elegant Letter*.
- **Lua errors on screen.** Type `/console scriptErrors 1` once on each client, or run BugSack, so no error passes silently.
- **Three spare keys.** Keys you use for nothing else: two to bind in step 8 and one in step 14. Clear the bindings when you finish, since a stray press of the erase key erases.
- **Items and bags.** Two spare grays, each a single item rather than a stack. More gray trash with a sell price, including three stacks of the same gray. A gray a vendor will not buy. A white weapon or armor piece a vendor will buy, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*. A white profession tool, *Mining Pick* or *Fishing Pole*, and a white shirt such as *Brown Linen Shirt*. An item your client draws in green. White trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*. *Refreshing Spring Water*, *Ice Cold Milk* and *Melon Juice*. *Rough Arrow*, *Sharp Arrow*, *Razor Arrow* and *Jagged Arrow*. A single stack of gray junk worth more than one gold. A stack of *Fish Oil*. A bound item that starts a quest and is already spent, meaning either the quest is done or your race or class can never take it. A quest item for a quest you have not finished. A **Rare (blue) item** a vendor will buy, which step 22 only drops and cancels. A bag sitting in one of your bank's bag slots. Keep a few free bag slots.
- **Guard your fixtures.** Every erase takes whatever the mini-map button names under **Lowest-Value Item**, which may be a fixture rather than a gray. Before any Left-Click or key press, check the name: if a later step still needs that item, Right-Click the button to protect it, and Middle-Click to clear the Protect List once the step is done. Only steps 20 and 21 sell or retrieve, and they come after every step that needs a flagged fixture.
- **Location and state.** Out of combat except where a step says otherwise. A merchant and a bank you can reach, ideally close together, and something willing to fight you for step 12.
- **A non-English client**, for the optional last step only.

Every step below has a pass or fail you can see. To stay short enough to finish in one sitting, this plan deliberately skips the welcome and mini-map toggles, profile switching beyond step 1, Restore Defaults on the Erase List, the Bag-Space Warnings countdown, Auto-Vend waiting out combat, Validate Data on four of its six files, the mini-map position readout, and the Event Log, Event Registration, Display Context, Saved Variables, Library Versions and Taint Log tools.

## Verify this release's changes

Everything in this section is new since 2026.09.18.A, the last tagged release, so this is the part worth running carefully.

### Settings carried over from the last release

**1.** Do this first, while **2026.09.18.A** is still installed. Open **Safety Features**, turn on **Enable Mini-map Eraser Confirmation**, and check only **For Completed Quest Items** and **For Gray Vendor-Trash Items**. Turn **Auto-Vend** off and **Maximum Value to Erase** on. Put one item on each of your two characters' own Ignore Lists, a different item on each, and note both. Log out, update to this build, and log in. **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Profiles** must show **Default** on both characters. Auto-Vend must still be off and the value cap still on. On **Erasing**, **Completed Quest Items**, **Dead-End Quest Starters** and **Gray Trash** must read **Erase, Ask First**, and the other three must read **Erase**. Each character's **Protect List** must hold its own item, and only its own. Turn Auto-Vend back on, log in on the other character, and it must be on there too. Last, in **Profiles**, make a new profile named *Bank Alt* on one character and turn Auto-Vend off in it: the other character must still have Auto-Vend on, and switching the first back to **Default** must leave its Protect List untouched. Failure is a character not on Default after the update, any setting you noted reset to its default, an old confirmation lost or landing on the wrong kind, a list emptied or merged into the other character's, or a one-off profile's setting leaking to a character on Default.

### Item data split by flavor

**2.** Open **Diagnostic Tools**, turn on **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, and click **Show Eraser Context**. The report's first line must end `// Flavor Vanilla // Data Vanilla` on Classic Era, `// Flavor Camelot // Data Camelot` on WoW Forever, `// Flavor TBC // Data TBC` on TBC Anniversary, and `// Flavor Vanilla (Season of Discovery) // Data Discovery` on a Season of Discovery character. Under **Validate Data** there must be six buttons: **Validate Quest-Items**, **Validate Quest-Starting-Items**, **Validate Consumables**, **Validate Ammo**, **Validate Equipment** and **Validate Class-Reagents**. Click **Validate Class-Reagents**: the box must count `Validated N / M IDs` for a moment, then show the same first line, a blank line, a header row starting `STATUS`, `SOURCE`, `ITEM_ID`, `NAME`, and two rows, *Shiny Fish Scales* and *Fish Oil*, each reading `OK` and `SHAMAN` under `DATA_CLASS`. Click **Validate Ammo**: there must be no `TABLE MISSING` row; note any `NOT ON CLIENT` count for your report, which is data for the next release rather than this step failing. Failure is a first line naming the wrong flavor or data folder, `Flavor ?`, a missing button, a box stuck on its progress count, any `TABLE MISSING` row, or a Lua error. **Season of Discovery is the one to watch**: it runs on the Classic Era client, so only this report shows it picked up its own data.

### The options overhaul

**3.** Open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser**. The tree under it must read, in order: **Erasing**, **Merchant & Bank**, **Alerts & Tooltips**, **Protect List**, **Erase List**, **Your Current Bags**, **Profiles**, **Diagnostic Tools**, with no **Safety Features**. On the General page, top to bottom: the description, **Enable Welcome Message** and **Enable Mini-map Button**, a **Features** header with six switches two to a line (**Enable Auto-Vend**, **Enable Bank Retrieval**, **Enable Manual Delete Assistance**, **Enable Tooltip Warnings**, **Enable Quest Item Alerts**, **Enable Bag-Space Warnings**), **Key Bindings**, **/Commands**, then **Feedback & Support** and the version. Turn **Enable Tooltip Warnings** off here, open **Alerts & Tooltips**, and its own switch must be off too; turn it back on there and the General page must match. Failure is a page missing or out of order, a switch on its own line, or the two copies of a switch disagreeing.

**4.** Open **Erasing**. Under **What Counts as Junk** must be an intro pointing to the Protect List and Erase List, then six rows (**Completed Quest Items**, **Dead-End Quest Starters**, **Outgrown Food & Drink**, **Outgrown Arrows & Bullets**, **White Weapons & Armor**, **Gray Trash**), each a dropdown of **Erase**, **Erase, Ask First** and **Keep**. Set **Gray Trash** to **Keep**: every gray must lose its tooltip line and never be named under **Lowest-Value Item**. Set all six to **Keep**: a red line must say only your Erase List is erased or sold. Set them all back to **Erase**. Below, **Enable Maximum Value to Erase** and its dropdown must share one line, the dropdown hidden while the box is off. Turn it on: the dropdown must read **1 Gold, 2 Gold, 3 Gold, 5 Gold, 8 Gold, 13 Gold, 21 Gold** in that order. Pick **1 Gold** with your junk stack worth more than a gold in your bags: without reloading, its tooltip must lose its Magic Eraser line and it must never be named under **Lowest-Value Item**. Leave the cap on for step 20. Failure is a kept kind still flagged, "13 Gold" anywhere but between "8 Gold" and "21 Gold", or the capped stack still offered.

**5.** With junk in your bags, open **Your Current Bags**. Under **Clutter Report** the first row must be the same item the mini-map button names, with its stack size, value and reason (**Gray**, **Outgrown**, **Quest Done**, **Can't Take**, **White Gear** or **Erase List**), then **Erase** and **Protect** buttons. Under **Up Next** must be up to five more items in the order the eraser will take them: with your three stacks of the same gray, the top row shows one stack and Up Next shows the other two as one row reading `x40 (2 stacks)` or similar, with their combined value. Last comes a **Total** line whose bag slots, items and gold match the mini-map tooltip's Clutter Report. Click **Erase**: the item must go with the usual chat line, and the page must move on to the next item by itself. Under **Everything in Your Bags**, every distinct item you carry must be listed once, sorted by name, with its type and an **Erase** and a **Protect** checkbox. Tick **Protect** on your *Hearthstone*, then **Erase** on the same row: Protect must untick and the item must move to the Erase List. Untick Erase. With nothing left to erase, the top of the page must read **Congratulations, your bags are full of good stuff!** with the full list still below it. Failure is an order that disagrees with the mini-map button, a page that does not redraw after an erase, both boxes ticked on one row, or a Lua error.

**6.** Open **Merchant & Bank**. **Enable Auto-Vend** and its chat dropdown must share one line, the dropdown offering **Summary in Chat**, **Every Sale in Chat** and **No Chat Report** and hidden while the switch is off. Below it, an example must follow your choice: one summary line, a sale line then a summary, or nothing at all. Turn **Enable Bag-Space Warnings** on in **Alerts & Tooltips** with **Free-Slot Threshold** at 4 and come back: **Bank Retrieval** must read `Leaves 4 bag slots free, because Bag-Space Warnings are on.`, and the line must go when you turn the warnings off. In **Alerts & Tooltips**, **Tooltip Warnings** must show three examples, `May be erased.` and `On your Erase List, and may be erased.` in red and `On your Protect List.` in white, and **Quest Item Alerts** two, each gone while its own switch is off. Failure is an example that does not follow its control, or the cushion line showing while the warnings are off.

**7.** Open **Protect List**. The tree must list **All Characters**, then each character with a list, then the one you are playing. Pick an item from **Add from Bags**: it must join the list, leave the picker, and the picker must read empty again. Click that row's **All Characters** button: it must leave this character's pane and appear under **All Characters**. Log in on your second character, carry the same item: its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // On your Protect List.` in white, and it must never be named under **Lowest-Value Item**. Back on the first character, open **Erase List** and add an item that is on your Protect List: its row must read **Protected** beside it. Remove both entries when done. Failure is a picker listing items already on the list, a promoted row showing in both panes, the second character not protecting it, or a missing **Protected** tag.

### Hovered-item key bindings

**8.** In the game's key binding list, find the **Magic Eraser** section and bind two spare keys to **Add Hovered Item to Protect List** and **Add Hovered Item to Erase List**. Point the mouse at empty ground and press either key: chat must print `Magic Eraser // Hover over an item, then press the key again.` Hover *Linen Cloth* in your bags and press the Erase List key: chat must print `Added [Linen Cloth] to your Erase List.` with a working item link, and **without moving the mouse** its tooltip must gain a red `Magic Eraser // On your Erase List, and may be erased.` line within a moment. Press it again: `[Linen Cloth] is already on your Erase List.` Press the Protect List key: `Moved [Linen Cloth] from your Erase List to your Protect List.`, and the line must turn to a white `Magic Eraser // On your Protect List.` Press the Erase List key once more: `[Linen Cloth] is on your Protect List, which always wins. Remove it from there first.` Hover a piece of equipped gear on your character sheet and press the Protect List key: it must be added the same way. Both must show under this character in **Protect List**, neither under **All Characters**. Remove both before moving on, since step 18 needs the trade good on neither list. Failure is a key that does nothing, a missing or doubled chat line, a tooltip that catches up only after you move the mouse away and back, or a Lua error. **WoW Forever reads the hovered item a different way from the other clients**, so a pass on one flavor proves nothing about the others.

### New junk rules: arrows and white gear

**9.** Carry *Rough Arrow*, *Sharp Arrow*, *Razor Arrow* and *Jagged Arrow* on characters at the right levels: *Rough Arrow* must be flagged from level 10, *Sharp Arrow* from 25, *Razor Arrow* from 40, and none of them one level earlier. *Jagged Arrow* must never be flagged on Classic Era, Season of Discovery or WoW Forever, at any level, and on TBC Anniversary only from level 55. Set **Outgrown Arrows & Bullets** to **Keep** on **Erasing**: every arrow must lose its tooltip line. Set it back. Then the white-gear rule: *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe* must carry a red `Magic Eraser // May be erased.` line, while your white profession tool and your white shirt must carry none and never be named under **Lowest-Value Item**. Failure is an arrow flagged a level early, a Jagged Arrow flagged where nothing better is sold, or a profession tool or shirt flagged.

When steps 1 to 9 pass on every flavor, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**10.** Log in with the add-on freshly enabled. Exactly one branded line must print, naming a version and pointing at Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser. On an unpackaged development copy the version reads `Dev`, which is correct; a packaged release must read a real version number on every flavor. Then `/reload`. Failure is no message, a doubled message, a literal `%s` where the version belongs, or any Lua error on either load.

**11.** Reach the Options Interface all three ways. Type `/eraser`. Close it and hold Shift while Middle-Clicking the mini-map button. Close it again, open the Blizzard Options window yourself and pick **Magic Eraser** out of the category list. All three must land on the same page, **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, and under the **/Commands** header `/eraser` must be listed in blue followed by **Opens the Options Interface for this add-on.** Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a run on Classic Era alone has not finished this step.

**12.** Get into combat and type `/eraser`. One line must read `Magic Eraser // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat.` and the panel must **not** open. Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button while still in combat: same line, same refusal. Ask three or four more times each way, and the line must print **every** time. Still in combat, with junk in your bags, Left-Click the button: chat must print `Magic Eraser // Cannot erase items while in combat.` and nothing may be erased. Then let combat end without touching anything: the panel must not open by itself and nothing may be erased late. Failure is the panel opening, an item vanishing, silence after the first refusal, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Magic Eraser.

**13.** Hover the mini-map button with several flagged items of different values in your bags. The tooltip must read, top to bottom: the add-on name with the version on the right; **Lowest-Value Item** with the item's icon, link and value; **Left-Click / Erase**; **Right-Click / Protect**; **Auto-Vend** showing Enabled or Disabled, with **Shift + Right-Click / Toggle**; **Clutter Report** with a bag-slot count, an item count and a total; the **Protect List** with **Middle-Click / Clear Protect List**, if you have anything protected; and last, **Magic Eraser Options** with **Shift + Middle-Click**. The button's icon must match the named item, and that item must be the cheapest **whole stack**: twenty grays at 1 copper are worth 20 copper and come after a single 5 copper item. Now work the clicks. Left-Click erases the named item, plays a sound, prints `Erased [item], worth [price].` with a working item link, and refreshes the tooltip **while you are still hovering**. Right-Click adds the named item to this character's Protect List block. Middle-Click empties that block. Shift + Right-Click flips Auto-Vend between Enabled and Disabled live, and **Enable Auto-Vend** on the General page must match; leave it Enabled. Failure is a block missing or out of order, a dearer stack offered first, a click doing something other than its label, or a tooltip that catches up only after you move the mouse away and back.

**14.** On the General page, under **Key Bindings**, three rows must sit in blue: **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, **Add Hovered Item to Protect List** and **Add Hovered Item to Erase List**, each followed by its key or a gray **Not Bound** and a **Set Key** button. Click **Set Key**: the game's key binding list must open, holding a **Magic Eraser** section with exactly those three entries. Bind your last spare key to **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, come back, and the row must show that key without a reload. Hover the mini-map button, note the named item, move the mouse away, and press the key: that item must go exactly as a Left-Click erases it, with the same chat line. Then set **Gray Trash** to **Erase, Ask First** on **Erasing**, close the Options window, and press the key with a gray named: an `Erase [item]?` dialog must appear. Click **No**, and the gray must still be in your bags. Set **Gray Trash** back to **Erase**. Failure is a missing row, a raw name such as `MAGICERASER_ERASE`, a **Set Key** that opens nothing, a different item going than the one named, or a key press erasing anything a Left-Click would have asked about first. If **Set Key** prints `Open the game menu, then Options, then Key Bindings, and find the Magic Eraser section.` instead, note the flavor and paste the three Set Key rows from step 23's API report.

**15.** Check the outgrown-drink boundary, on one character you can level or on three at the right levels: *Refreshing Spring Water* is flagged from level 5, *Ice Cold Milk* from 15, *Melon Juice* from 25, and none of the three one level below that. Then carry a drink one level short of qualifying and **level up**: the candidate must update on the level-up itself, with you touching nothing. Failure is a boundary landing somewhere else, or having to move an item before the add-on notices.

**16.** Pick a quest chain that hands you an item a later quest still needs; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar are the quickest. Work it up to the quest **before** the last one that needs the item, turn that one in, and accept the next so the item is back in your bags. Stop there and wait a few seconds: chat must print **nothing** about the item, its tooltip must carry **no** Magic Eraser line, and it must never be named under **Lowest-Value Item**. Type `/reload` and check all three again. Now turn in the last quest that needs it. If the item is still in your bags, within about a second chat must print exactly one line reading `[item] can now be safely erased. It's left over from a quest you have completed.` (with ` x2` after the link for a stack of two), its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // May be erased.`, and it must become the candidate; Left-Click must print `Erased [item], left over from a quest you have completed.` If the turn-in used the item up, silence is correct. Failure before that last turn-in is the dangerous one: it means the add-on is offering to erase a quest item the character is about to need.

**17.** Put a spent quest starter in your bank, `/reload`, and open the bank so Bank Retrieval hands it back; looting a fresh one works too. Within about a second of it landing in your bags, chat must print exactly one line: `[item] can now be safely erased. It's left over from a quest you have completed.` when the quest is already done, or `[item] can now be safely erased. It starts a quest your character can't take.` when your race or class is locked out. Move it between bag slots, then close and reopen the bank: it must **not** print again. Turn **Enable Quest Item Alerts** off, put it back in the bank, `/reload` and reopen the bank: no line at all, but it must still be named under **Lowest-Value Item** once it is the cheapest. Turn the alerts back on. Left-Click it: chat must read `Erased [item], which starts a quest your character cannot take.` for a lock-out, and `Erased [item], left over from a quest you have completed.` for a quest you finished. Separately, on a character who **can** take that quest and has not done it, carry a copy: no alert, no tooltip line, never the candidate. Failure is silence with the alerts on, a line with them off, a repeat on every bag change, the wrong sentence for the reason, or, worst of all, the eligible character being offered a quest starter it still needs.

**18.** Confirm what is never touched, one item at a time: an item your client draws in **green**, white trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*, your equipped gear, a quest item for a quest you have not finished, and a gray a vendor will not buy. None may show a Magic Eraser line and none may ever be named under **Lowest-Value Item**; keep the bag items with you for step 20, where none may sell. Pick a trade good that is **not** on your Erase List, since the seeded fishing reagents are white trade goods the add-on is supposed to take. Run the green half on **every** flavor with the same item, because a handful of low-level crafted pieces are white on one client and green on another, and the client in front of you decides. Failure here is the most dangerous kind of bug this add-on can have.

**19.** On a character that is **not** a Shaman, open **Erase List**. That character's own pane must already list **Shiny Fish Scales** and **Fish Oil**. Carry your *Fish Oil* stack: its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // On your Erase List, and may be erased.` in red. Remove Shiny Fish Scales with its remove icon: it must go with one click and no confirmation, and must not come back after a `/reload`. Log in on a Shaman and check the same pane: it must list **neither** reagent. Failure is an empty list on a fresh non-Shaman, a Fish Oil stack with no red line, a removed row coming back after a reload, or a Shaman starting with their own reagents listed.

**20.** Take your bags to a merchant with **Enable Auto-Vend** on and its chat dropdown set to **Every Sale in Chat**. Everything flagged that a vendor will buy must sell on opening, including the junk stack worth more than a gold with step 4's cap still on. Each sale prints one line reading `Sold [item], worth [price].` with a working, quality-colored item link you can click, and closing the merchant window prints `Sold N items (M bag slots), worth X.` Left in your bags must be everything from step 18's never-touched set, your white profession tool and shirt, and anything on your Protect List. Turn the cap off. Switch the dropdown to **Summary in Chat**, buy one gray back from **Buyback**, close the window, and talk to the merchant again: on closing, only `Sold 1 item (1 bag slot), worth X.` may print. Failure is a broken or half-rendered link, a stray `%s`, a line that is not one complete sentence, "1 items" or "1 bag slots", a per-item line under Summary in Chat, the capped stack left unsold, or anything protected or never-touched sold.

**21.** Put your two spare grays in your bank, one in its own slots and one in the bag sitting in a bank bag slot, and close it. Open it again: both must come back under one line, `Pulled 2 items (2 bag slots) out of your bank, worth X.` Then put more flagged items in your bank than you have free slots, turn **Enable Bag-Space Warnings** on with **Free-Slot Threshold** at 4, and open the bank: retrieval must stop with at least four slots still free. Failure is a gray left behind, no line in chat, or a bank that ignores the threshold while the warnings are on. **WoW Forever is the flavor to watch**, since its bank is the one most likely to be laid out differently.

**22.** Open **Erasing** and note the word the **Manual Delete Assistance** paragraph quotes. With **Enable Manual Delete Assistance** on and the dropdown beside it on **Items with No Sale Value**, drag your sellable Rare item out of your bags and drop it on the ground: the game's own prompt must appear untouched, asking you to type that same word, letter for letter. Press **No**. Switch the dropdown to **All Items** and drop the item again: the prompt must now be a plain Yes or No, with no typing box and a **Yes** button you can click. Press **No**, and the item must still be in your bags. Set the dropdown back to **Items with No Sale Value**. Failure is a different word than the game's prompt, the typing box surviving under All Items, a grayed-out Yes, or the item gone after No. **WoW Forever is the flavor where this is least proven.**

**23.** `/reload` and open **Diagnostic Tools**. **Enable Diagnostic Tools** must be **off**, with only the warning paragraph and that toggle visible. Turn it on and click **Test WoW API Endpoints**. Note every `[FAIL]` row on each flavor for your report; a FAIL alone is not this step failing, because several rows are alternatives where one half is enough. This step fails if any of these reads FAIL: a `C_Container` row, `C_Item.GetItemInfo`, `C_Item.GetItemInfoInstant`, `C_Item.GetItemSpell` or `C_Item.GetItemStats` (white gear is never erased without the last two), `DELETE_GOOD_ITEM`, `DELETE_ITEM_CONFIRM_STRING` or any of the four `StaticPopup1` rows (step 22 depends on them). It also fails if **both** halves of a pair read FAIL: `TooltipDataProcessor.AddTooltipPostCall` with `GameTooltip.SetBagItem (legacy)`, `TooltipUtil.GetDisplayedItem` with `GameTooltip.GetItem (legacy)`, or `C_QuestLog.GetTitleForQuestID` with `C_QuestLog.GetQuestInfo`; or if all three Set Key routes, `Settings.KEYBINDINGS_CATEGORY_ID`, `SettingsPanel.GetCategoryList` and `KeyBindingFrame_LoadUI (legacy)`, read FAIL. Then `/reload` and reopen the panel: the toggle must be back **off**. Failure is also the toggle surviving a reload, or a Lua error from the button.

**24.** Optional, on a non-English client: open the Options Interface, walk every Magic Eraser page, find the **Magic Eraser** section in the game's key binding list, hover the mini-map button, and erase and sell one item. Every string must render as real words in that language, with no raw keys such as `TAB_ERASING` showing through. Strings new in this release may still show in English until the Localization pass, which is expected, and every number and item name must land inside its sentence rather than leaving a bare `%s` or `%d` behind. The **Manual Delete Assistance** paragraph must quote the same word that client's own delete prompt asks for, and the closing line for selling one item must read naturally for a single item. Failure is a raw key, a stray placeholder, a sentence assembled in the wrong order, or a quoted word the client's own prompt does not use.

When every step passes on Classic Era, WoW Forever, TBC Anniversary and Season of Discovery, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
