# Magic Eraser // Test Plan

Magic Eraser spots the junk in your bags and erases it a click at a time, or sells it at your next merchant. This time we most want you on the mini-map button's new click, Auto-Repair, and the new junk lines in item tooltips.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Left-click the mini-map button: the menu opens and stays open until you left-click again, and no other click on the button does anything.
2. Look over the mini-map menu: prices show coin icons, and no row names a mouse click any more.
3. Turn on Auto-Repair under Merchant & Bank and visit a merchant who repairs: your gear is fixed and chat says what it cost, unless you hold Shift as the merchant opens.
4. Hover junk in your bags: the tooltip says what kind of junk it is, and Erased Next on the one an Erase would take.
5. Hover an item on your Protect List: the tooltip says which list keeps it safe.
6. Turn on Bag-Space Warnings and fill your bags to the last slot while carrying junk: chat says how many slots are junk.

## Everything Else

7. Click Erase in the mini-map menu to remove your Lowest-Value Item, then Protect the next one.
8. Open your bags and bank, then loot junk in combat on WoW Forever: only junk is tinted, even as it lands.
9. Open your bank through a bag add-on such as Baganator: Bank Retrieval brings your junk into your bags.
10. Open a merchant and let Auto-Vend sell your junk, then hold Shift on the next visit: nothing sells.
11. Turn in the last quest that needs an item still in your bags: chat tells you it's now safe to erase.
12. Drag a Rare item no merchant will buy onto the ground: you get a plain Yes or No.
13. Bind **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, **Add Hovered Item to Protect List** and **Add Hovered Item to Erase List** under Key Bindings and try each one.
14. Type `/eraser`, then click Magic Eraser Options at the bottom of the menu: both open the options inside the game's Options window, and TBC Anniversary is where this breaks.
15. In combat, try `/eraser`, and Magic Eraser Options and Erase in the menu: all three refuse.
