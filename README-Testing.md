# Magic Eraser // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Magic Eraser, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on Classic Era, then `/reload` and run it again on TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these first, so you are not caught short halfway through:

- **Characters.** One character at level 15 or higher on each flavor, who is **not** a Shaman. A second character on the same account for the Global list check in step 19, and a Shaman on the same account for step 20. For step 16 you want a character partway through one of the quest chains named there; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore for Alliance and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar for Horde both open before level 10. For step 17 you want a character who is locked out of a quest by race or class, and one who is not: any non-Human for *Goldshire Gift Voucher*, any non-Tauren for *Bloodhoof Village Gift Voucher*, any non-Rogue for *Elegant Letter*. Step 8 also wants a level 55 or higher character carrying a spare *Argent Dawn Commission*; without one, run that step's first half only.
- **Items and bags.** Gray trash with a sell price. A curated white, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*. An item your client draws in green. White trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*. *Refreshing Spring Water*, *Ice Cold Milk* and *Melon Juice*. A single stack of junk worth more than one gold. A stack of *Fish Oil*. A bound item that starts a quest and is already spent, meaning either the quest is done or your race or class can never take it. A quest item for a quest you have not finished. For steps 4 and 5, a **Rare (blue) item you are willing to lose** that a vendor will buy, and, if you happen to own one, a Rare or better item no vendor will buy, such as an armor token. Keep a few free bag slots.
- **Location and state.** Out of combat except where a step says otherwise. A merchant and a bank you can reach, and something willing to fight you for step 11.
- **A non-English client**, for the optional last step only.

Every step below has a pass or fail you can see. To stay short enough to finish in one sitting, this plan deliberately skips the erase confirmation dialog's own Yes and No behavior, the welcome and mini-map toggles, profile switching, adding items by ID inside either list panel, Restore Defaults on the Erase List, the Bag-Space Warnings countdown, Auto-Vend deferring a sale until combat ends, and the Validate Data reports beyond the one spot-check in step 8.

## Verify this release's changes

Everything in this section is new since 2026.09.01.A, the last packaged build, so this is the part worth running carefully.

### Settings split across two panels

**1.** Type `/eraser`. The settings must appear **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Magic Eraser selected in the category list on the left and its children listed as **Safety Features**, **Ignore List**, **Erase List**, **Profiles**, **Diagnostic Tools**, in that order. The page you land on must now hold only these, top to bottom: the add-on's description, **Enable Welcome Message**, **Enable Mini-map Button**, the **/Commands** header with `/eraser` in blue beneath it, the **Auto-Vend** section, and **Feedback & Support** with the muted version line under it. Everything else that used to sit here has moved to Safety Features. Failure is a missing or misordered child, a setting still on this page that step 2 also finds on Safety Features, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. This release removed the add-on's old fallback route into the panel, so a floating window now means the one remaining route is not working, and **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**.

**2.** Click **Safety Features**. It must open with one paragraph about item restoration, then six sections in this order: **Tooltip Warnings**, **Bank Retrieval**, **Manual Delete Assistance**, **Mini-map Eraser Confirmation**, **Maximum Value to Erase**, **Bag-Space Warnings**. On a fresh profile the first three must be **on** and the last three **off**. Failure is a section in the wrong place or missing, or a default landing the other way, which would mean the add-on ships more trigger-happy, or more timid, than intended.

**3.** Still on Safety Features, work the toggles that hide something, one at a time. **Enable Manual Delete Assistance**, already on, must show a single indented row reading **Applies To** in silver beside a dropdown. **Enable Mini-map Eraser Confirmation** must reveal four indented tick boxes, one per line, reading **For Completed Quest Items**, **For Outgrown Consumable Items**, **For White Vendor-Quality Items**, **For Gray Vendor-Trash Items**. **Enable Maximum Value to Erase** must reveal **Never Erase Anything Worth More Than** beside a dropdown, and **Enable Bag-Space Warnings** must reveal **Free-Slot Threshold** beside a slider. Every one of those rows must sit on **one** line with its caption, and turning the toggle back off must take the whole row away, indent included. Then open **Ignore List** and **Erase List** and confirm the **Add by Item ID** caption and its box also share one line. The panel grid was widened this release, so wrapping is the thing to watch for: failure is a control dropping onto its own line, an indent left stranded above nothing, or a caption running into the control beside it.

### Manual Delete Assistance

**4.** Drag a Rare (blue) item that a vendor will buy out of your bags and drop it on the ground. On the shipped setting the game's own dialog must still make you type **Delete** before its accept button lights up, because the default only covers items no vendor will buy. Press **Cancel**. Now set **Applies To** to **All Items** and drag the same item out again: the dialog must come up with **no** typing field, **no** line telling you to type anything, an accept button already lit, and a box sized to what is left rather than one with a hole in it. Press **Cancel** again. If you own a Rare or better item no vendor will buy, set **Applies To** back to **No Sale Value** and drag that one out: it must come up as a plain Yes or No too. Failure is a typing field still there under All Items, an accept button that never lights, or a dialog that lost its wording along with its field.

**5.** Open a delete dialog again and leave it alone for a few seconds. The item must still be in your bags and the dialog must still be waiting: the add-on never answers the prompt for you, so nothing may go until **you** click the accept button yourself. Click it, and the item goes, once. Then untick **Enable Manual Delete Assistance** and drag a Rare item out again: the typed prompt must come back exactly as the game ships it. Failure is an item disappearing without your click, a dialog that closes itself and leaves the item behind, or a simplified prompt surviving after you turn the feature off.

### Renamed settings

**6.** Read the renamed strings back on **Safety Features**. The first section must be headed **Tooltip Warnings**, not "Item Tooltips", and its toggle must read **Enable Tooltip Warnings**. The fourth must be headed **Mini-map Eraser Confirmation**, not "Eraser Confirmations", with its toggle to match, and its sub-toggles must read **For Outgrown Consumable Items** and **For Gray Vendor-Trash Items**, not "Low-Level" and "Gray Vendor-Quality". Hover the **Add by Item ID** box on either list panel and the tip must say **Shift-click**, with a capital S. Failure is any old wording surviving, on this flavor or the other. The one chat line that changed with them is checked in step 16.

### One alert per item

**7.** Find an item in your bags that starts a quest, accept that quest, complete it, and turn it in while the item is still in your bags. Within about a second chat must print `[item] can now be safely erased!` **exactly once**. Count the lines. Failure is that line arriving twice in a row, which is the bug this release fixes. If the turn-in takes the item out of your bags there is nothing left to announce and silence is correct, so pick a different starter and run the step again.

### Argent Dawn Commission

**8.** Open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, and click **Validate Quest-Items.lua**. When the report finishes counting, search it for id **12846**, *Argent Dawn Commission*: on both flavors that row must read `OK`. Then, if you have a level 55 or higher character carrying a spare Commission, check the new row in the world. Before you finish *The Active Agent* in the Eastern Plaguelands the Commission must not be flagged: no Magic Eraser line on its tooltip, and never the candidate on the mini-map button. Turn that quest in, and within about a second chat must print `[item] can now be safely erased!` and its tooltip must carry `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.` Failure is `NOT ON CLIENT` on the id, or a Commission offered for erasure before that quest is done.

When steps 1 to 8 pass on both flavors, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**9.** Log in with the add-on freshly enabled. Exactly one branded line must print, naming a version and pointing at Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser. On an unpackaged development copy the version reads `Dev`, which is correct; a packaged release must read a real version number. Then `/reload`. Failure is no message, a doubled message, a literal `%s` where the version belongs, or any Lua error on either load.

**10.** Reach the Options Interface all three ways. Type `/eraser`. Close it and hold Shift while Middle-Clicking the mini-map button. Close it again, open the Blizzard Options window yourself and pick **Magic Eraser** out of the category list. All three must land on the same docked page, and under the **/Commands** header `/eraser` must be listed in blue followed by **Opens the Options Interface for this add-on.** Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a run on Classic Era alone has not finished this step.

**11.** Get into combat and type `/eraser`. One line must read `Magic Eraser // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat.` and the panel must **not** open. Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button while still in combat: same line, same refusal. Ask three or four more times each way, and the line must print **every** time. Still in combat, Left-Click the button with junk in your bags: one line must read `Magic Eraser // Cannot erase items while in combat.` and nothing may be erased. Then let combat end without touching anything: the panel must not open by itself and nothing may be erased late. Failure is the panel opening, an item vanishing, silence after the first refusal, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Magic Eraser.

**12.** Hover the mini-map button with junk in your bags. The tooltip must read, top to bottom: the add-on name with the version on the right; **Lowest-Value Item** with the item's icon, link and value; **Left-Click / Erase**; **Right-Click / Ignore**; **Auto-Vend** showing Enabled or Disabled, with its description and **Shift + Right-Click / Toggle**; **Clutter Report** with a bag-slot count, an item count and a total; the **Ignore List** with **Middle-Click / Clear Ignore List** below it, if you have anything ignored; and last, **Magic Eraser Options** with **Shift + Middle-Click**. The button's icon must match the item named under Lowest-Value Item. Failure is a missing block, a block out of that order, an empty line where a value belongs, or an icon that does not match.

**13.** Work through the clicks and confirm each does what the tooltip beside it says. Left-Click erases the named item, plays a sound, prints `Erased [item], worth [price].` with a working item link, and refreshes the tooltip to the next item **while you are still hovering**. Right-Click adds the named item to this character's Ignore List, and it appears in the tooltip's Ignore List block. Middle-Click empties that block. Shift + Right-Click flips the Auto-Vend line between Enabled and Disabled live, and **Enable Auto-Vend** on the General page must be flipped to match. Failure is any click doing something other than its label, the two Auto-Vend readings disagreeing, a broken link in the erase line, or a tooltip that catches up only after you move the mouse away and back.

**14.** With several flagged items of different values in your bags, confirm the candidate really is the cheapest, comparing whole stacks rather than single items: twenty grays at 1 copper is worth 20 copper and outranks a single 5 copper item. Then make two flagged items worth the same and check the tie-break, which runs Erase List items first, then completed quest items, then gray trash, then consumables and curated whites together. Failure is a more valuable stack being offered first, or a tie broken the other way.

**15.** Check the outgrown-consumable boundary, on one character you can level or on three at the right levels: *Refreshing Spring Water* is flagged from level 5, *Ice Cold Milk* from 15, *Melon Juice* from 25, and none of the three one level below that. Then carry a drink one level short of qualifying and **level up**: the candidate must update on the level-up itself, with you touching nothing. Failure is a boundary landing somewhere else, or having to move an item before the add-on notices.

**16.** Pick a quest chain that hands you an item a later quest still needs; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar are the quickest. Work it up to the quest **before** the last one that needs the item, turn that one in, and accept the next so the item is back in your bags, since several of these chains take the item at one turn-in and hand it straight back for the next. Stop there and wait a few seconds: chat must print **nothing** about the item, its tooltip must carry **no** Magic Eraser line, and it must never be named under **Lowest-Value Item** or wear its icon on the button. Type `/reload` and check all three again. Now turn in the last quest that needs it. If the item is still in your bags, within about a second chat must print exactly one line reading `[item] can now be safely erased!`, its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.`, and it must become the candidate; Left-Click must print `Erased [item], left over from a quest you have completed.` If the turn-in used the item up, silence is correct. Failure before that last turn-in is the dangerous one: it means the add-on is offering to erase a quest item the character is about to need.

**17.** With the add-on already loaded, put a spent quest starter into your bags, by looting one or pulling one out of the bank. Within about a second chat must print exactly one line: `[item] can now be safely erased!` when the quest is already complete, or `[item] can be safely erased. It starts a quest your character cannot take.` when your race or class is locked out. Move it between bag slots, then open and close a merchant: it must **not** print again. Hover the mini-map button and Left-Click once it is the named candidate: chat must read `Erased [item], which starts a quest your character cannot take.` for a lock-out, and `Erased [item], left over from a quest you have completed.` for a quest you finished. Now carry that same race-locked or class-locked item on a character who **can** take the quest and has not done it: no alert, no tooltip line, never the candidate. Failure is silence, a repeat line on every bag change, the wrong sentence for the reason, or, worst of all, the eligible character being offered a quest starter it still needs.

**18.** Confirm what is flagged and what never is. Put a curated white in your bags, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*: its name must be white, and its tooltip must carry a red `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.` line. Then work through the never-touched set one item at a time: an item your client draws in **green**, white trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*, your equipped gear, a quest item for a quest you have not finished, and a gray a vendor will not buy. None may be flagged, none may show a Magic Eraser line, and none may sell at a merchant. Pick a trade good that is **not** on your Erase List, since the seeded fishing reagents are white trade goods the add-on is supposed to take. Run the green half on **both** flavors with the same item, because a handful of low-level crafted pieces are white on one client and green on the other, and the client in front of you decides. Failure here is the most dangerous kind of bug this add-on can have.

**19.** Right-Click the mini-map button to ignore the current candidate, then confirm it is skipped everywhere: its tooltip reads `Magic Eraser // Protected by your Ignore List.` in white, it never becomes the candidate, Auto-Vend leaves it alone at a merchant, and Bank Retrieval leaves it in the bank. Open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Ignore List**, find that row under this character and click its **Global** button: the row must leave this character's pane and appear under **Global**. Log in on a second character with its own Ignore List empty: the same item must still be protected everywhere. Failure is a protected item being erased, sold or retrieved on either character, or the promoted row showing up in both panes.

**20.** Log in on a character that is **not** a Shaman and open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Erase List**. That character's own scope must already list **Shiny Fish Scales** and **Fish Oil**, each drawn as a real item link with its icon rather than a raw ID. Click the **Global** button on Shiny Fish Scales: that row must leave this pane and turn up under **Global**. Remove it there with its remove icon: it must go with one click and no confirmation, and must not come back to either pane after a `/reload`. Log in on a Shaman and check the same pane: it must list **neither** reagent. Failure is an empty list on a fresh non-Shaman, a promoted row showing in both panes, a removed row coming back after a reload, or a Shaman starting with their own reagents listed.

**21.** With *Fish Oil* still on that non-Shaman's Erase List, carry a stack. Its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // Flagged by your Erase List.` in red, it can become the candidate, it sells at a merchant, and it walks out of the bank. Turn on **Maximum Value to Erase** with the limit set below that stack's total value: the listed item must **still** be flagged, because the cap does not apply to a list you built. Last, add the same item to this character's **Ignore List** as well: the tooltip must flip to `Magic Eraser // Protected by your Ignore List.` and the item must be spared everywhere, even though it now sits on both lists. Failure is the cap suppressing a listed item, or the Erase List winning over the Ignore List.

**22.** Open **Safety Features** and find **Maximum Value to Erase**. Turn it on and set the dropdown, which must read **1 Gold, 2 Gold, 3 Gold, 5 Gold, 8 Gold, 13 Gold, 21 Gold** in that order, to **1 Gold**, with a stack of junk in your bags worth more than a gold. Without reloading, the mini-map icon and **Lowest-Value Item** must move to something cheaper, the **Clutter Report** must drop by that stack, and the stack's tooltip must show **no** Magic Eraser line. Then take the stack to a merchant with Auto-Vend on: it must still sell, and a second one in your bank must still walk back into your bags. Failure is "13 Gold" sitting between "1 Gold" and "2 Gold", the button still wearing that stack's icon, or the cap blocking a sale or a retrieval.

**23.** Sell a batch at a merchant with **Enable Auto-Vend Messages** on and the mode set to **Line Item**. Each sale prints one line reading `Sold [item], worth [price].` with a working, quality-colored item link you can click, and closing the merchant window prints one more line reading `Sold N items (M bag slots), worth X.` Switch to **Summary Only**, the shipped default, and sell another batch: only the closing line prints. Then open a bank holding flagged junk: one line must read `Pulled N items (M bag slots) out of your bank, worth X.` On a fresh profile, with Bag-Space Warnings off, retrieval may use every free slot you have. Turn **Enable Bag-Space Warnings** on with **Free-Slot Threshold** at 4 and open the bank again: this time retrieval must stop with at least four slots still free. Every one of these lines must read as a complete sentence. Failure is a broken or half-rendered link, a stray `%s`, a per-item line in Summary Only mode, or a bank that ignores the threshold once the warnings are on.

**24.** Open **Diagnostic Tools**. On a fresh login **Enable Diagnostic Tools** must be **off**, with only the warning paragraph and that toggle visible. Turn it on and click **Test WoW API Endpoints**. Every line must read PASS, with one exception: the four modern and legacy pairs, the three `C_AddOns` pairs and the tooltip pair, where at least one half must pass and a FAIL on the other half is the report working rather than a defect. Note which half passed for each pair, on each flavor. `Settings.OpenToCategory` is no longer half of a pair and must read PASS on its own, `InterfaceOptionsFrame_OpenToCategory` must be gone from the list entirely, and the seven rows Manual Delete Assistance added, `StaticPopup_FindVisible`, `DELETE_GOOD_ITEM`, `DELETE_ITEM_CONFIRM_STRING` and the four `StaticPopup1` methods, must all read PASS. Then `/reload` and reopen the panel: the toggle must be back **off**. Failure is the toggle surviving a reload, both halves of any pair reading FAIL, or a FAIL on any line outside those pairs.

**25.** Optional, on a non-English client: open the Options Interface, walk the General and Safety Features pages, hover the mini-map button, and erase and sell one item. Every string must render as real words in that language, with no raw keys such as `TAB_SAFETY` showing through, and every number and item name must land inside its sentence rather than leaving a bare `%s` or `%d` behind. Failure is a raw key, a stray placeholder, or a sentence assembled in the wrong order.

When every step passes on both Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
