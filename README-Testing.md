# Magic Eraser // Test Plan

Magic Eraser spots the junk in your bags and erases it a click at a time, or sells it at your next merchant. This time we most want you on the new mini-map menu, which replaces the old tooltip.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Hover the mini-map button to open its menu, try the Erase and Protect rows under your Lowest-Value Item, then press Escape to close it.
2. Protect a few items, then click one in the menu's Protect List to take it off; Clear Protect List asks before it empties the list.
3. Click Toggle under Auto-Vend and flip a switch under More Features: the menu and an open options page both follow.
4. After updating, check your mini-map button is where you left it, then switch profiles: each keeps its own button spot and Enable Mini-map Button setting.
5. Loot meat and fish well below your level: they stay until you tick Outgrown Meat & Fish under Erasing, while fish you can eat that no recipe uses, such as Bloodbelly Fish, goes with Outgrown Food & Drink.

## Everything Else

6. Open your bags and bank, with your bag add-on if you use one, then loot junk in combat on WoW Forever: only junk is tinted, even as it lands.
7. Open a merchant and let Auto-Vend sell your junk: nothing green or better and no quest item you still need ever sells.
8. Open your bank and let Bank Retrieval bring your junk home, watching WoW Forever most closely.
9. Turn in the last quest that needs an item still in your bags: chat tells you it's now safe to erase.
10. Bind **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, **Add Hovered Item to Protect List** and **Add Hovered Item to Erase List** under Key Bindings and try each one.
11. Drag a Rare item no merchant will buy onto the ground: you get a plain Yes or No instead of typing DELETE.
12. Type `/eraser`, then click Magic Eraser Options at the bottom of the menu: both open the options inside the game's Options window, and TBC Anniversary is where this breaks.
13. In combat, try `/eraser`, and Magic Eraser Options and Erase in the menu: all three refuse.
