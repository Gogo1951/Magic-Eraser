# Magic Eraser // Test Plan

Magic Eraser spots the junk in your bags and erases it a click at a time, or sells it at your next merchant. This time we most want you on quest items from long quest chains, and on switching Auto-Vend off in the middle of a sale.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. On a Horde character, carry the Befouled Water Globe through The Befouled Element: it should only be offered once you've handed in Je'neu of the Earthen Ring.
2. Make sure Evergreen Pouch, Dawn's Gambit, Blood of Innocents and Zorbin's Ultra-Shrinker are never offered, even after their quests are done.
3. While Auto-Vend is selling a big pile of junk, Shift + Right-Click the mini-map button: the selling should stop straight away.
4. Right after logging in, open the Protect List and Erase List: every item should show its name, in your character's list and the All Characters list alike.
5. On WoW Forever, check that the mini-map button's ring and icon sit as neatly as the game's own buttons.

## Everything Else

6. Hover the mini-map button to see your cheapest junk, then Left-Click to erase it or Right-Click to protect it.
7. Open a merchant and let Auto-Vend sell your junk: nothing green or better, no white trade goods such as Linen Cloth, and no quest item you still need should ever sell.
8. Open your bank and let Bank Retrieval bring your junk home, watching WoW Forever most closely.
9. Turn in the last quest that needs an item still in your bags: chat should tell you it's now safe to erase.
10. Bind **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, **Add Hovered Item to Protect List** and **Add Hovered Item to Erase List** under Key Bindings and try each one, on WoW Forever too.
11. On a Shaman, open the Erase List: none of your own reagents, such as Fish Oil or Ankh, should be on it.
12. Drag a Rare item no vendor will buy onto the ground: you should get a plain Yes or No instead of typing DELETE.
13. Type `/eraser`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button: both should open the options inside the game's Options window, and TBC Anniversary is where this breaks.
14. In combat, try `/eraser`, Shift + Middle-Click and Left-Click on the mini-map button: all three should refuse.
