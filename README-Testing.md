# Magic Eraser // Test Plan

Magic Eraser spots the junk in your bags and erases it a click at a time, or sells it at your next merchant. This time we most want you on the new junk tint in your bags and bank, with whichever bag add-on you play with.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Open your bags and bank with the default bags, then with Baganator, Bagnon, AdiBags, ArkInventory, BetterBags or ElvUI if you use one: only the junk Magic Eraser would erase is tinted blue.
2. Right-Click the mini-map button to protect your Lowest-Value Item, then add something good with **Add Hovered Item to Erase List**: the tint moves with each.
3. Loot junk in combat, on WoW Forever too: it's tinted as it lands.
4. Under Alerts & Tints, change the Tint Color and its opacity, then turn off Enable Junk Item Tint: your open bags change straight away.
5. Press Clear List on another character's Erase List and Protect List, then log in as them: both stay empty.
6. On Classic Era, press Restore Defaults on a Warrior's Erase List, then on a Mage's or Priest's: Light Feather joins only the Warrior's.

## Everything Else

7. Hover the mini-map button to see your cheapest junk, then Left-Click to erase it or Right-Click to protect it.
8. Open a merchant and let Auto-Vend sell your junk: nothing green or better, no white trade goods such as Linen Cloth, and no quest item you still need ever sells.
9. Open your bank and let Bank Retrieval bring your junk home, watching WoW Forever most closely.
10. Turn in the last quest that needs an item still in your bags: chat tells you it's now safe to erase.
11. Bind **Erase Lowest-Value Item**, **Add Hovered Item to Protect List** and **Add Hovered Item to Erase List** under Key Bindings and try each one, on WoW Forever too.
12. Drag a Rare item no vendor will buy onto the ground: you get a plain Yes or No instead of typing DELETE.
13. Type `/eraser`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button: both open the options inside the game's Options window, and TBC Anniversary is where this breaks.
14. In combat, try `/eraser`, Shift + Middle-Click and Left-Click on the mini-map button: all three refuse.
