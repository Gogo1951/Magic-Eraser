# Magic Eraser // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Magic Eraser, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Magic-Eraser/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on Classic Era, then `/reload` and run it again on TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these first, so you are not caught short halfway through:

- **Characters.** One character at level 15 or higher on each flavor, who is **not** a Shaman. A second character on the same account for the Global list check in step 15, and a Shaman on the same account for step 16. For steps 1 and 2 you want a character partway through one of the quest chains listed there; *Bashal'Aran* in Darkshore for Alliance and *Hidden Enemies* in Orgrimmar for Horde both open before level 10. For step 12 you also want a character who is locked out of a quest by race or class, and one who is not: any non-Human for *Goldshire Gift Voucher*, any non-Tauren for *Bloodhoof Village Gift Voucher*, any non-Rogue for *Elegant Letter*.
- **Items and bags.** Gray trash with a sell price. A curated white, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*. An item your client draws in green. White trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*. *Refreshing Spring Water*, *Ice Cold Milk* and *Melon Juice*. A single stack of junk worth more than one gold. A stack of *Fish Oil*. A bound item that starts a quest and is already spent, meaning either the quest is done or your race or class can never take it. A quest item for a quest you have not finished. Keep a few free bag slots.
- **Location and state.** Out of combat except where a step says otherwise. A merchant and a bank you can reach, and something willing to fight you for step 7.
- **A non-English client**, for the optional last step only.

Every step below has a pass or fail you can see. To stay short enough to finish in one sitting, this plan deliberately skips the erase confirmation dialog, the welcome and mini-map toggles, profile switching, adding items by ID inside either list panel, Restore Defaults on the Erase List, Bag-Space Warnings, and Auto-Vend deferring a sale until combat ends.

## Verify this release's changes

Everything in this section is new since the last packaged build, so this is the part worth running carefully.

### Quest items that stay protected until their chain is done

Twelve entries in the quest-item list were tied to a quest that comes **before** the last quest to need the item. On those chains the add-on would call a quest item safe to erase while the character still needed it, and a Left-Click on the mini-map button would have destroyed it. Each of the twelve is meant to stay protected until the quest named here is complete.

| Item | Safe only once this quest is complete | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ancient Moonstone Seal | *Bashal'Aran*, part 4 of 4, the one that has you destroy the seal at Ameth'Aran | Darkshore, Alliance, from level 7 |
| Lieutenant's Insignia | *Hidden Enemies*, part 2 of 5, the visit to Neeru Fireblade | Orgrimmar and Durotar, Horde, from level 9 |
| Filled Etched Phial | *The Sacred Flame*, part 3 of 3, at Splithoof Hold | Thousand Needles, Horde, from level 20 |
| Field Testing Kit | *Tanaris Field Sampling*, where the kit goes back to Chief Engineer Bilgewhizzle with the samples | Tanaris, Horde, from level 39 |
| Purified Moonwell Water | *Dousing the Flames of Protection* | Felwood, both factions, from level 48 |
| Good Luck Half-Charm | *Two Halves Become One* | Western Plaguelands, both factions, from level 50 |
| Black Dragonflight Molt | *A Taste of Flame*, the Blackrock Depths part, after both Burning Steppes parts of the same name | Blackrock Depths, both factions, from level 52 |
| Thorium Shackles | *Precarious Predicament*, the escort that hands the shackles to Lexlort | Burning Steppes to Searing Gorge, Horde, from level 52 |
| Soulbound Keepsake | *The Lich, Ras Frostwhisper* | Scholomance, both factions, from level 57 |
| Left Piece of Lord Valthalak's Amulet | *The Right Piece of Lord Valthalak's Amulet*, any of its four dungeon versions, where the pieces are recombined | Blackrock Mountain, both factions, from level 58 |
| Human Orphan Whistle | *A Warden of the Alliance*, the last Children's Week quest, which takes the whistle back | Stormwind, Alliance, Children's Week only |
| Blood Oath of the Horde | *All Hail Roanauk!* | Northrend. It cannot appear on Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, so there is nothing to run |

**1.** Pick a chain from the table you can reach; *Bashal'Aran* and *Hidden Enemies* are the quickest. Work it up to the quest **before** the named one, turn that one in, and accept the named quest so the item is in your bags, since several of these chains take the item at one turn-in and hand it straight back for the next. Stop there. Wait a few seconds: chat must print **nothing** about the item. Hover it in your bags: there must be **no** Magic Eraser line on its tooltip. Hover the mini-map button: it must not be named under **Lowest Value Item** and its icon must not be on the button. Type `/reload` and check all three again. Failure is an alert line, a tooltip line reading `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.`, or the item ever becoming the candidate. This is the dangerous one: it means the add-on is offering to erase a quest item the character is about to need.

**2.** Carry on and turn in the quest named in the table. If the item is still in your bags afterward, within about a second chat must print exactly one line reading `[item] can now be safely erased!`, its tooltip must now carry `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.`, and it must become the candidate on the mini-map button. Left-Click: chat must read `Erased [item], from a quest you have completed.` If the quest itself used the item up, there is nothing left to erase and silence is the correct result. Failure is an item still in your bags that never gets flagged, the alert printing more than once, or the erase line claiming the item was worth money.

### Validate Data in Diagnostic Tools

**3.** Open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Diagnostic Tools** and tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**. Below **Eraser Context** there must now be four sections headed **Validate Data: Quest-Items.lua**, **Validate Data: Quest-Starting-Items.lua**, **Validate Data: Consumables.lua** and **Validate Data: Equipment.lua**, each with its own **Validate** button and empty output box, and one silver paragraph explaining the STATUS column under the last of them. Click **Validate Equipment.lua**, the largest. The box must first show a line in the shape `Validated 300 / 1,023 ...` under the client header, counting up, and within about ten seconds be replaced by the finished report: the client header, one tally line naming the file and how many ids read OK, NOT ON CLIENT and NOT LOADED, a blank line, a header row beginning `STATUS`, `SOURCE`, `ITEM_ID`, `NAME`, then one row per item id. Click into the box, select all, copy, and paste into a spreadsheet: every value must land in its own column, with item links arriving as plain text. Failure is a missing section, a button that does nothing, a progress line that never resolves, a Lua error, or a report whose links render as clickable items instead of pasting as text.

**4.** Click **Validate Quest-Items.lua** and search the finished report for **Blood Oath of the Horde**, id 35784. On Classic Era that row, and every other row in the Burning Crusade and Wrath sections of the file, must read `NOT ON CLIENT`, while the Classic rows read `OK`. On TBC Anniversary the Burning Crusade rows flip to `OK` and only the Wrath rows stay flagged. Then click **Validate Equipment.lua** again and, while it is still counting, untick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**: everything below the toggle must vanish at once. Tick it back on: the Equipment box must be empty, not stuck on a stale progress line, and nothing may carry on counting on its own. Failure is an `OK` on an id your client cannot know, a `NOT LOADED` across a whole block of rows, or a progress line that survives the toggle.

When steps 1 to 4 pass on both flavors, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**5.** Log in with the add-on freshly enabled. Exactly one branded line must print, naming a version and pointing at Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser. On an unpackaged development copy the version reads `Dev`, which is correct; a packaged release must read a real version number. Then `/reload`. Failure is no message, a doubled message, a literal `%s` where the version belongs, or any Lua error on either load.

**6.** Type `/eraser`. The settings must appear **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Magic Eraser selected in the category list on the left. Close it and hold Shift while Middle-Clicking the mini-map button: the same docked page must open. Close it again, open the Blizzard Options window yourself and pick **Magic Eraser** out of the category list. All three routes must land on the same docked page, its children must be listed as **Ignore List**, **Erase List**, **Profiles**, **Diagnostic Tools** in that order, and under the **/Commands** header `/eraser` must be listed in blue followed by **Opens the Options Interface for this add-on.** Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, or a standalone window floating free of the Options frame. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this**, so a run on Classic Era alone has not finished this step.

**7.** Get into combat and type `/eraser`. One line must read `Magic Eraser // As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat.` and the panel must **not** open. Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button while still in combat: same line, same refusal. Ask three or four more times each way, and the line must print **every** time. Still in combat, Left-Click the button with junk in your bags: one line must read `Magic Eraser // Cannot erase items while in combat.` and nothing may be erased. Then let combat end without touching anything: the panel must not open by itself and nothing may be erased late. Failure is the panel opening, an item vanishing, silence after the first refusal, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error naming Magic Eraser.

**8.** Hover the mini-map button with junk in your bags. The tooltip must read, top to bottom: the add-on name with the version on the right; **Lowest Value Item** with the item's icon, link and value; **Left-Click / Erase**; **Right-Click / Ignore**; **Auto-Vend** showing Enabled or Disabled, with its description and **Shift + Right-Click / Toggle**; **Clutter Report** with a bag-slot count, an item count and a total; the **Ignore List** with **Middle-Click / Clear Ignore List** below it, if you have anything ignored; and last, **Magic Eraser Options** with **Shift + Middle-Click**. The button's icon must match the item named under Lowest Value Item. Failure is a missing block, a block out of that order, an empty line where a value belongs, or an icon that does not match.

**9.** Work through the clicks and confirm each does what the tooltip beside it says. Left-Click erases the named item, plays a sound, prints `Erased [item], worth [price].` with a working item link, and refreshes the tooltip to the next item **while you are still hovering**. Right-Click adds the named item to this character's Ignore List, and it appears in the tooltip's Ignore List block. Middle-Click empties that block. Shift + Right-Click flips the Auto-Vend line between Enabled and Disabled live, and **Enable Auto-Vend** in the Options Interface must be flipped to match. Failure is any click doing something other than its label, the two Auto-Vend readings disagreeing, a broken link in the erase line, or a tooltip that catches up only after you move the mouse away and back.

**10.** With several flagged items of different values in your bags, confirm the candidate really is the cheapest, comparing whole stacks rather than single items: twenty grays at 1 copper is worth 20 copper and outranks a single 5 copper item. Then make two flagged items worth the same and check the tie-break, which runs Erase List items first, then completed quest items, then gray trash, then consumables and curated whites together. Failure is a more valuable stack being offered first, or a tie broken the other way.

**11.** Check the outgrown-consumable boundary, on one character you can level or on three at the right levels: *Refreshing Spring Water* is flagged from level 5, *Ice Cold Milk* from 15, *Melon Juice* from 25, and none of the three one level below that. Then carry a drink one level short of qualifying and **level up**: the candidate must update on the level-up itself, with you touching nothing. Failure is a boundary landing somewhere else, or having to move an item before the add-on notices.

**12.** With the add-on already loaded, put a spent quest starter into your bags, by looting one or pulling one out of the bank. Within about a second chat must print exactly one line: `[item] can now be safely erased!` when the quest is already complete, or `[item] can be safely erased, from a quest your character cannot take.` when your race or class is locked out. Move it between bag slots, then open and close a merchant: it must **not** print again. Hover the mini-map button and Left-Click once it is the named candidate: chat must read `Erased [item], from a quest your character cannot take.` for a lock-out, and `Erased [item], from a quest you have completed.` for a quest you finished. Now carry that same race-locked or class-locked item on a character who **can** take the quest and has not done it: no alert, no tooltip line, never the candidate. Failure is silence, a repeat line on every bag change, the wrong sentence for the reason, or, worst of all, the eligible character being offered a quest starter it still needs.

**13.** Put a curated white in your bags, *Cracked Leather Vest* or *Rough Broad Axe*. Its name must be white, and its tooltip must carry a red `Magic Eraser // Will be erased.` line. Then find an item your client draws in **green** and confirm it is never flagged: no tooltip line, never the candidate, never sold. Run the green half on **both** flavors with the same item, because a handful of low-level crafted pieces are white on one client and green on the other, and the client in front of you decides. Failure is a curated white being skipped, or any green item being offered for erasure.

**14.** Confirm the never-touched set, one item at a time: white trade goods such as *Linen Cloth*, anything green or better, your equipped gear, a quest item for a quest you have not finished, and a gray a vendor will not buy. Pick a trade good that is **not** on your Erase List for this one, since the seeded fishing reagents are white trade goods the add-on is supposed to take. None may be flagged, none may show a Magic Eraser line on its tooltip, and none may sell at a merchant. Failure here is the most dangerous kind of bug this add-on can have.

**15.** Right-Click the mini-map button to ignore the current candidate, then confirm it is skipped everywhere: its tooltip reads `Magic Eraser // Protected by your Ignore List.` in white, it never becomes the candidate, Auto-Vend leaves it alone at a merchant, and Bank Retrieval leaves it in the bank. Open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Ignore List**, find that row under this character and click its **Global** button: the row must leave this character's pane and appear under **Global**. Log in on a second character with its own Ignore List empty: the same item must still be protected everywhere. Failure is a protected item being erased, sold or retrieved on either character, or the promoted row showing up in both panes.

**16.** Log in on a character that is **not** a Shaman and open **Options > AddOns > Magic Eraser > Erase List**. That character's own scope must already list **Shiny Fish Scales** and **Fish Oil**, each drawn as a real item link with its icon rather than a raw ID. Click the **Global** button on Shiny Fish Scales: that row must leave this pane and turn up under **Global**. Remove it there with its remove icon: it must go with one click and no confirmation, and must not come back to either pane after a `/reload`. Log in on a Shaman and check the same pane: it must list **neither** reagent. Failure is an empty list on a fresh non-Shaman, a promoted row showing in both panes, a removed row coming back after a reload, or a Shaman starting with their own reagents listed.

**17.** With *Fish Oil* still on that non-Shaman's Erase List, carry a stack. Its tooltip must read `Magic Eraser // Flagged by your Erase List.` in red, it can become the candidate, it sells at a merchant, and it walks out of the bank. Turn on **Maximum Value to Erase** with the limit set below that stack's total value: the listed item must **still** be flagged, because the cap does not apply to a list you built. Last, add the same item to this character's **Ignore List** as well: the tooltip must flip to `Magic Eraser // Protected by your Ignore List.` and the item must be spared everywhere, even though it now sits on both lists. Failure is the cap suppressing a listed item, or the Erase List winning over the Ignore List.

**18.** Type `/eraser` and find the **Maximum Value to Erase** section. On a fresh profile **Enable Maximum Value to Erase** must be **off** with no limit row beneath it. Turn it on: one indented row must appear, the silver caption **Never Erase Anything Worth More Than** with a dropdown beside it reading **1 Gold, 2 Gold, 3 Gold, 5 Gold, 8 Gold, 13 Gold, 21 Gold** in that order. Pick **1 Gold** with a stack of junk in your bags worth more than a gold. Without reloading, the mini-map icon and **Lowest Value Item** must move to something cheaper, the **Clutter Report** must drop by that stack, and the stack's tooltip must show **no** Magic Eraser line. Then take the stack to a merchant with Auto-Vend on: it must still sell, and a second one in your bank must still walk back into your bags. Failure is the toggle shipping on, "13 Gold" sitting between "1 Gold" and "2 Gold", the button still wearing that stack's icon, or the cap blocking a sale or a retrieval.

**19.** Sell a batch at a merchant with **Enable Auto-Vend Messages** on and the mode set to **Line Item**. Each sale prints one line reading `Sold [item], worth [price].` with a working, quality-colored item link you can click, and closing the merchant window prints one more line reading `Sold N items (M bag slots), worth X.` Switch to **Summary Only** and sell another batch: only the closing line prints. Then open a bank holding flagged junk: one line must read `Pulled N items (M bag slots) out of your bank, worth X.`, and retrieval must stop before filling you up, leaving at least the number of free slots set under **Bag-Space Warnings**, four on a fresh profile. Every one of these must read as a complete sentence. Failure is a broken or half-rendered link, a stray `%s`, a per-item line in Summary Only mode, or a bank that fills you to your last slot.

**20.** Open **Diagnostic Tools**. On a fresh login **Enable Diagnostic Tools** must be **off**, with only the warning paragraph and that toggle visible. Turn it on and click **Test WoW API Endpoints**. Every line must read PASS, except inside the four modern and legacy pairs, the two add-on metadata lines, the two options-opener lines, the two tooltip lines and the two item-lookup lines, where at least one half must pass and a FAIL on the other half is the report working rather than a defect. Note which half passed for each pair, on each flavor. Then `/reload` and reopen the panel: the toggle must be back **off**. Failure is the toggle surviving a reload, both halves of any pair reading FAIL, or a FAIL on any line outside those pairs.

**21.** Optional, on a non-English client: open the Options Interface, hover the mini-map button, and erase and sell one item. Every string must render as real words in that language, with no raw keys such as `TAB_IGNORE_LIST` showing through, and every number and item name must land inside its sentence rather than leaving a bare `%s` or `%d` behind. Failure is a raw key, a stray placeholder, or a sentence assembled in the wrong order.

When every step passes on both Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
